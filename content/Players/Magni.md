# 🧔 Magni Darkforge
## Magni Darkforge
* **Race:** Mountain Dwarf
* **Class:** Barbarian Path of the Totem Warrior

## The Tempest of Khaz'modan
[[Magni]] was born within the great halls of [[Khaz Modan]], into the hardened [[Darkforge Clan]]. This clan, comprised of miners, smiths, and warriors, worshipped the forge and the fury of the mountain, prizing endurance, strength, and honor. However, [[Magni]] was different. While his kin found comfort in the traditions of forge and stone, [[Magni]] heard the call of the wilds—the howling winds, the crash of thunder, and the primal rage that surged within his blood.

Even as a child, he was a tempest: quick to laugh, quicker to anger, and impossible to control. Only his mother, [[Elmear]], and younger brother, [[Duramir Darkforge]], knew how to quell his ferocity. While others studied runes and hammers, [[Magni]] would vanish into the high passes for days, returning covered in blood and earth, eyes wild with tales of mountain beasts and visions in blood. His rage wasn't just emotional; it was elemental, almost sacred. The elders called it a curse. [[Magni]] called it power.

After some time, his Clan decided to journey to [[Valdrak]], to make a name for themselves and push their clan to the pinnacle of dwarven kind. The [[Darkforge Clan]] once thrived deep within the heart of [[Valdrak]], known for crafting weapons imbued with rare ores and magical properties, drawn from veins tainted by ancient, sleeping powers. Having established a place within [[Valdrak]], [[Magni]] and his family flourished and lived everyday to its fullest, until the [[#Great Sundering]].

## The Sundering and Exile
[[Magni]] was a warrior then, a loyal son and promising heir. He fought for the clan’s honour and bled for its ambition. But when the city of [[Valdrak]] came under attack, it was too late. Shadows clung to flesh. The dead screamed through the stone. The clan’s hold turned into a charnel house in a matter of minutes.

[[Magni]] survived, though he doesn’t remember how. His memories are splintered: blood on his axe, kin turned to twisted things, voices whispering in a language he shouldn’t understand. When he awoke, the lands were unfamiliar, and everyone was gone. Everyone but him.

When [[Magni]] finally made his way to familiar dwarven lands, he staggered into a nearby dwarven settlement, half-mad and covered in gore. They branded him an exile. Murderer. Cursed. Even he doesn’t fully deny it because sometimes, when he rages in battle, he hears the voices again, feels the presence of something deep and old stirring within his soul. He left the mountain of his own will, realizing he would never find peace among those who saw him as a freak.

## A Wandering Storm
Now, [[Magni]] wanders the continent, not seeking redemption, but vengeance for those lost lives that day. He is a wandering storm. He drinks deep, laughs loud, and fights harder than any man alive. But beneath his unruly beard and scarred knuckles lies a heart still chained to his lost clan and fallen brother. Every battle is a prayer, every roar an echo of the mountain he left behind. He’s not just angry; he’s haunted. And part of him fears that, in the end, he is the thing that killed them all.

Two years have passed since then. [[Magni]] is now a mercenary who'll take any job if the price is right, killing those who do or don't deserve such a fate. Those who face the fury of 'The Bloody Handed' meet a grim and gruesome end.

After completing another job, [[Magni]] heads to the taverns for drinks and food. After some time of drinking, he overhears people talking of an elf wanting a guide/bodyguard for their travels. Against his better, drunken judgment, he decides to find this elf for some easy coin.
