**Appearance:** This book is a living artifact. Its covers are crafted from smooth, silver-white bark that feels warm to the touch, and it is bound with woven, still-green vines that sprout tiny, luminescent mosses. The "pages" are not paper, but impossibly thin, pressed leaves, fused together into a seamless whole. The runes within are not written in ink, but glow with a soft, golden-green light, as if traced in pure life-essence. A faint, pleasant scent of fresh rain, rich soil, and blooming night-flowers emanates from its pages.

**The Vision Within:** This book contains a poetic, prophetic vision of a healed [[Kaeldor/WorldInfo/Kaeldor]].

> _"Behold the Heartwood's bloom. After the Long Winter and the reign of the Ashen King, a seed of hope, long dormant in the heart of the world, shall awaken. From the soil, purified by sacrifice and watered by the tears of a redeemed queen, a great Tree shall rise, its boughs scraping the heavens and its roots singing in the deep places of the earth._
>  _Its light shall push back the shadows. The blighted lands shall turn green, the poisoned rivers shall run clear, and the sky, once choked with the dust of undeath, shall be brilliant blue. The Tree shall connect all that lives; the elf in her forest hall, the dwarf in his mountain home, and the man in his city of stone will feel the pulse of its life within their own breasts. It will be an age of unparalleled peace, where the song of the Verdant Canticle is the only anthem, and the whispers of the Empty Star are drowned out by the rustle of a million leaves."_

This tome presents a utopian future, one where the party is successful, and the World Tree becomes the ultimate symbol of their victory over the forces of undeath and corruption. It is a vision of pure hope.
