# The Dwarven Faction: The Stone-Exiles
* **Origin:** The [[The Stone-Exiles|The Stone-Exiles]] are not a native faction of [[Aeris Gard]]. They are the remnants of the powerful dwarven mining guilds and diplomatic envoys from [[Khaz Modan]] who were residing in the capital when the [[#Great Sundering]] occurred. The raising of [[Aeris Gard]] and the creation of the [[Aegis of Aeris]] was, for them, an act of damnation. They are now trapped on a floating island, permanently severed from their mountain homes, their kin, and the very stone that is their birthright.
* **Leadership & Goal:** Led by the calculating and deeply resentful Guildmaster [[Borin Stonehand]], the [[The Stone-Exiles|The Stone-Exiles]] operate from the industrial underbelly of the floating city. While they maintain a facade of loyalty, their true goal is to seize political and structural control of [[Aeris Gard]]. They believe [[King Illhoof]], in his desperation, doomed them to a life in the sky—an unnatural prison for a dwarf. Their ultimate plan is to amass enough power to force the King to find a way to lower the city, viewing him and all of humanity as incompetent custodians of their fate. They leverage their mastery of engineering and metalcraft to control vital parts of the city's infrastructure, working to make the throne entirely dependent on them.
