# The King's Tutors
The men and women tasked with forging a secret boy into a king for a broken world.
* **Politics & Law: [[Chamberlain Valerius]].** An elderly, severe man who has served the Fenmir dynasty his entire life. He witnessed the kingdom's Golden Age and its fall, leaving him cynical and utterly pragmatic. His lessons are harsh, focusing on the brutal realities of power, the necessity of sacrifice, and the history of betrayals—both from enemies and within the court—that [[Blake]] must be prepared for.
* **History & Lore: [[Loremaster Rhiannon]].** A stoic historian who lost her family during the war against the [[Lich King]]. Her teachings are not grand tales of glory but somber case studies of loss, military failure, and the catastrophic consequences of the [[#Great Sundering]]. She ensures [[Blake]] understands the true cost of his father's decisions and the weight of the crown he is destined to inherit.
* **Magic: [[Magister Kaelan]].** A former [[Veil Warden]], the order of mages who enacted the [[#Great Sundering]]. [[Kaelan]] is grim and disciplined, teaching magic not as an art, but as a weapon and a tool. He sees [[Blake]]’s innate talent as a dangerous, volatile force that must be brutally controlled and honed for the single purpose of defending the kingdom.
* **Swordsmanship & Tactics: [[Ser Joren]].** A towering, scarred knight who once commanded a garrison on the northern frontier before the [[#Great Sundering]]. He is quiet, patient, and methodical. He does not train [[Blake]] to be a tournament champion but a survivor, drilling him relentlessly in defensive postures and the brutal, efficient tactics needed to survive an assassination attempt in the close confines of the palace.
