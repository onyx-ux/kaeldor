## Session Start

### 1. Narrative Overview & Themes

The party emerges from the silent, hallowed halls of the Frozen Library not into an empty passage, but into the epicentre of a cataclysmic battle's final moments. They are witnesses to the end of an era, the last stand of the final guardian of [[Old Jotenheim]].

- **Core Dramatic Question:** When faced with the staggering aftermath of a hero's final sacrifice, what is the party's duty—to the dead, to the living, and to the future?
- **Themes:** A glorious death, the horror of undeath, the end of an era, sacrifice, and the passing of a torch. [[Ornn]] is not just a giant; he is a living monument, and his fall will resonate through the story.
- **Background Forces:** This was a targeted strike. The [[Lich King]] has learned of the Frozen Library's existence and dispatched one of his chief lieutenants, the newly forged "Shadowed Hand," to desecrate the site and retrieve its secrets. The battle the party witnesses is the failure of that mission, bought at a terrible price.

## Old Jotenheim and the Last Guardian

### Scene and Narrative, The Desecration of Memory

### The Antechamber of the End
You emerge from the passage leading from the mountain's peak into a vast, cavernous hall that serves as the entrance to the legendary Frozen Library. The scene before you is one of epic, recent carnage. The air is thick with the smell of ozone and the foul, coppery tang of necrotic ichor.

This chamber was a killing ground. The floor is a treacherous slick of frozen black fluid and splintered ice, littered with the hollowed-out, dormant husks of dozens of corrupted giants and their skeletal minions. The walls are scarred by immense weapons and seared by magic. The sheer scale of the assault is staggering, but the defenders were few. You see the bodies of only two or three other giants who fought and fell, their forms torn asunder.

Amidst the slaughter, a single set of massive tracks, slick with black blood, leads from the centre of the carnage deeper into the darkness of the Frozen Library's entrance. The trail speaks of a lone survivor, wounded but defiant, who has retreated into the sacred space beyond.

**(Interaction Point):** The party has discovered the aftermath of a massive battle. This is a crime scene.

- An **Investigation** or **Survival** check can confirm the tracks belong to a single, heavily injured giant who was fighting a desperate, retreating battle against overwhelming odds.
- An **Arcana** check on the husks confirms they are vessels for the [[Lich King]]'s plague of will—soulless puppets.
- The party must decide how to proceed. Do they follow the tracks of the lone survivor into the library? Do they take time to search the bodies of the fallen for clues?

### The Final Blow

The sounds of the desperate, final moments of a colossal battle guide you into the desecrated heart of the Frozen Library. You arrive at the central rotunda to a scene of epic tragedy. Standing over the fallen body of the **Shadowed Hand** is **[[Ornn]]**, a lone mountain of battered flesh and iron. His massive axe is buried in the chest of his unholy foe.

For a heartbeat, there is an absolute, victorious silence. Then, with a roar that shakes the very ice beneath your feet—a sound of sorrow, of duty, of grim finality—[[Ornn]] wrenches his axe free. The body of his brother, [[Vigmar]], collapses to the floor, finally still.

The victory is short-lived. A violent tremor shakes [[Ornn]]'s body as the violet, web-like veins of the plague flare hungrily from the wounds covering him, crawling up his arms like hungry vines. He cries out, a sharp intake of breath, not just in pain, but in violation. The plague, its external host destroyed, now turns its full attention to its new vessel.

But [[Ornn]] does not fall. He does not convulse. With a second roar, one of pure defiance, he slams his mailed fist into a nearby granite pedestal with such force that the stone cracks and the bones in his hand shatter with a sickening crunch. A flash of white-hot, real pain courses through him, and the violet light of the corruption visibly recedes, beaten back by a surge of pure agony and willpower. He stands, breathing in ragged, desperate gasps, his body a warzone, his will the only thing holding the line.

**(Interaction Point):** The party witnesses an incredible act of violent self-control. This is not a mindless monster or a dying victim; this is a legendary warrior engaged in the fight of his life against an enemy inside his own skin. His action is both terrifying and awe-inspiring. Do they approach with caution? Do they offer help? His state is volatile, a clear display of immense power and immense danger.

### A Testament Forged in Pain



### 2. Location & Setting Details

**The Antechamber of the End**

This is a vast, cavernous hall that serves as the entrance to the Frozen Library. It was once a place of grand ceremony; now, it is a tomb.

- **Sights:** The scene is one of absolute carnage. The chamber is a frozen field of death, littered with the shattered bodies of dozens of corrupted giants and their twisted minions—ice ghouls, skeletal behemoths, and wraiths of frost. The floor is a treacherous slick of frozen black ichor and splintered ice. Colossal pillars are cracked and broken, and the walls are scarred by titanic weapons and seared by necrotic magic. The air is thick with a black, oily smoke that smells of burned flesh and ozone, shimmering with the residual energy of the battle—sickly violet clashing with the heroic gold of [[Ornn]]'s fading power.
- **Sound:** The deafening roar of battle has just ceased. The only sounds are the groaning of stressed ice, the low, malicious hum of a cursed weapon, the ragged, final breaths of a dying hero, and the party's own hammering hearts.
- **The Focal Point:** In the centre of the carnage, a mountain of defiance stands: **[[Ornn]]**. He is leaning on his colossal axe, his body a ruin of shattered armour and gaping, glowing wounds. At his feet lies the immense, armoured corpse of his opponent.

### 3. Key NPCs

**[[Ornn]], Last Guardian of [[Old Jotenheim]]**

- **Description:** He is a figure of terrible majesty. His left arm hangs limp, and a massive shard of black ice is embedded in his shoulder. Every breath is a struggle. But he stands unbroken, his one good eye burning with defiant light. He has single-handedly held this chokepoint against a legion and won. He is the epitome of the giants' vow: better to break than to bend.   

**The Shadowed Hand (The corpse of [[Vigmar]])**

- **Description:** Though defeated, his presence is terrifying. He is a frost giant encased in jagged, night-black plate armour that seems fused to his flesh. His helm is shaped into a jagged crown of a dead king. Lying next to his outstretched hand is his weapon: a colossal greatsword of black, screaming crystal that pulses with a faint, violet light and emits a low, hateful hum.

### 4. Encounter: A Hero's End

As the party enters, they witness the final act. [[Ornn]], with a final, desperate roar, brings his axe down on the last of the skeletal behemoths, shattering it into a thousand pieces. The silence that follows is absolute. He stands for a moment, a lone peak in a valley of death, before his strength fails him and he sinks to one knee, his axe haft the only thing holding him up. His gaze finds the party, and there is no hostility in it, only a profound, soul-deep exhaustion.

- **The Dialogue:** His voice is a low rumble, cracked with pain.
    - "So... the silence is broken by small feet... at the very end." He gives a grim, bloody smile. "You are too late to join the fight... but just in time to witness the victory."
    - "My name is [[Ornn]]. And I was the last guardian of this sacred place. The memory of my people."
    - He gestures with his head towards the body of the Shadowed Hand. "He came for our stories. For the _Star-Shaper's Ledger_. My brother, [[Vigmar]], led them. Led this filth to our very heart. The [[Lich King]] hollowed him out... made him this... 'puppet'."    

- **The Choice:** He coughs, a spray of black blood staining the ice. The violet glow in his wounds intensifies.
    - "I have held the line. The library is safe. But his master's poison is in my veins. A slow, crawling rot..." A look of horror flashes in his eye. "I saved our memory. Do not let my reward... be an eternity as _that_."    
    - He unclasps a runestone from his belt—it is identical to the armour of his fallen brother. "This was his command stone. It holds his master's will. Take it to [[New Jotenheim]]. Let it be my warning. Now... grant a hero's final honour. Let my silence be eternal."


### 5. Hooks & Branching Choices

- **The Request (Mercy):** The choice is the same, but the stakes are higher. Killing him is no longer just mercy; it is the final rite for a legendary hero who just saved the party's most valuable discovery. His death would feel like the fall of a great tree.
    
- **Abandonment:** To leave him now would be a profound act of cowardice and disrespect. The consequence remains: they will face the undead hero [[Ornn]], a monster forged from the corpse of the man who died to protect the very knowledge they carry.
    
- **The Screaming Blade:** The greatsword of the Shadowed Hand lies on the ground. It is clearly an artifact of immense power and immense evil.
    
    - **If the party takes it,** they acquire a potent, cursed magic weapon. It might whisper to them, offer them power, and slowly corrupt them, a constant reminder of the [[Lich King]]'s influence.
        
    - **If they try to destroy it,** it might not be so simple, perhaps requiring a special ritual or forge, creating a new quest hook.
        

### 6. Secrets & Discoveries

- **The Enemy's Goal:** The party now knows for a fact that the [[Lich King]] is seeking specific knowledge about the "Empty Star" (The Wanderer), confirming the cosmic stakes of the conflict.
    
- **The Command Stone:** The runestone from the Shadowed Hand is a major discovery. It is not just a message; it's a piece of the enemy's command structure. It could be used to fool lesser undead, understand their plans, or might contain a direct link to the [[Lich King]]'s will, making it both valuable and incredibly dangerous.
