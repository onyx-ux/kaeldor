# Session 1
During this session, the party was met with [[Oswin]].

[[Oswin]] has been corrupted by the lich, [[Oswin]] was sent on his mission by the lich to [[Old Jotenheim]] as per “[[The Corruption Journey]]” & “[[The Fall of Ymir]]”

The party came across him and went on his way through to [[Old Jotenheim]] from this they were able to successfully defeat [[Oswin|King Oswin]] to the point that he had to leave during this fight when was being taken over by the [[Lich King|Valameer]] he spoke to the party, whispering things to the party.

After this encounter ended. The party made their way to the fairy terminal.
On their 2 week journey there, they made camp half way there.
During the night in the distance a carriage containing [[Kaan]], [[Alis]], [[Ser Joren]], [[Ser Korbin]] and [[Princess Anya]].

[[Visage]] noticed this and investigated the carriage. Out of this encounter the following occurred:

* [[Visage]] met [[Kaan]] and [[Alis]].
* [[Kaan]] and [[Alis]] set a warning to [[Visage]] not to come closer.
* [[Visage]] understood that there was a seal of some kind which he could not make out.
* [[Kaan]] is a rather ruff individual holding a great axe.
* He is very wary of travelers and is quick to anger
* [[Alis]] is rather cool heeded, still the same wariness however understands that you cannot be aggressive to everyone.

[[Visage]] Returning to the camp, informed the remainder of the party. [[Aolis]] in turn decided to investigate more.
He wildshapped into a rat and went to investigate further.

Out of this; the following information was made to [[Aolis]]:

* [[Princess Anya]] and her knight escort are heading to [[Old Jotenheim]].
* A description of [[Princess Anya]] and [[Ser Joren]].

[[Aolis]] then turned back and headed back to camp.
