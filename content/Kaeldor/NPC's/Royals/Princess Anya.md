# Princess Anya
## The Royal Sister: Princess Anya Fenmir

* **Age:** 19
* **Personality:** [[Princess Anya]] is a product of the grim twilight that followed the [[#Great Sundering]]. Publicly, she is the perfect heir apparent that can never be—poised, dutiful, and deeply empathetic to the suffering of her people. Privately, she is pragmatic to the point of ruthlessness, having learned from her father's failed idealism that sentiment is a luxury a ruler cannot afford. She carries the weight of the kingdom on her shoulders and harbors a quiet, fierce resentment for the laws that deny her the throne.
* **Role & Relation to Blake:** For years, [[Princess Anya|Anya]] was the sole heir of a broken king, the focus of all political scheming. The court's panic over the succession (fearing the Fenmir line would end with her) is what forced the King to reveal [[Blake]]'s existence. [[Princess Anya|Anya]]'s relationship with her suddenly-revealed younger brother is complex. She feels a genuine, protective affection for the boy who was raised in a cage, yet she cannot ignore that he is also the living symbol of her own disenfranchisement. She acts as his mentor in the treacherous currents of the royal court, all while weighing how his existence impacts her own plans for the kingdom's survival.
* **Description:** [[Princess Anya]] is a young woman who carries the weight of a kingdom in her gaze. She has the regal bearing of the Fenmir line, with a poise that seems almost unnatural for her nineteen years. Her attire, though practical for travel, is of fine make, hinting at her noble status. Her expression is often one of solemn duty and empathy for the suffering of her people, though a sharp, pragmatic intelligence shines through in her eyes. She is the image of a leader forced to mature far too quickly in a world teetering on the brink of another war.
