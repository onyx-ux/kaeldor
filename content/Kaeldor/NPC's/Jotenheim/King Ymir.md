### **The Plague of Will and the Hollow King**

The corruption of King [[King Ymir|Ymir]] is a spiritual plague. It is a contagion of the soul, transmitted by exposure to the [[Lich King]]'s concentrated will, such as through corrupted artifacts, powerful necrotic wounds, or prolonged proximity to a potent carrier.

**The Infection of King Ymir:** The vector was, as we established, the corrupted shard [[King Ymir|Ymir]] claimed as a trophy. But it was not a whispering tempter; it was the point of infection, like a rusty nail carrying a deadly disease. The plague entered [[King Ymir|Ymir]] and lay dormant, studying its host. It found a fertile ground in his pride, his ambition to restore the giants' honor, and his deep-seated frustration with the policy of hiding.

The initial symptoms were purely psychological. The plague amplified his existing personality traits into their most extreme forms.

- His **pride** became aggressive arrogance.
- His **ambition** became ruthless obsession.
- His **frustration** became paranoid rage.


He felt invigorated, purposeful, and stronger than ever. He mistook the symptoms of the disease for the birth of a glorious new conviction. This is the plague's insidious genius: it convinces its host that their descent into madness is actually an ascent into greatness.

**The "Semi-Death Knight" Transformation:** Now, the plague is entering its second stage. [[King Ymir|Ymir]] is being hollowed out. He is becoming a vessel, a "semi-death knight" in the truest sense, mirroring what happened to [[Oswin]].

- **Minimal Agency:** [[King Ymir|Ymir]] still possesses his memories, his personality, and his motivations. He wakes up every day believing he is King [[King Ymir|Ymir]], fighting for the future of his people. However, his will is no longer entirely his own. The plague has fundamentally rewritten his instincts and conclusions. He is still on the throne, but the [[Lich King]]'s will is the subtle gravity that guides his every decision. He will use his own logic, his own charisma, and his own strength to arrive at the exact conclusions the plague—and by extension, [[Lich King|Eldhoof]]  desires. He is a puppet who not only believes he is free but believes he is the strongest he has ever been.

- **Physical Manifestations:** The transformation is becoming visible. His skin has taken on a deathly, stone-grey pallor. The air around him is unnaturally cold. In moments of extreme emotion, his eyes burn with the faint, tell-tale violet light of the [[Lich King]]'s magic. He is not rotting or undead, but is being perfectly preserved and remade into a suitable container for overwhelming power.   

**The Outbreak in New Jotunheim:** [[King Ymir|Ymir]] has become "Patient Zero." His most loyal followers, the "Revivalists," are not just a political faction; they are the infected. Through sparring sessions, inspiring speeches, and close counsel, [[King Ymir|Ymir]] has unwittingly spread the metaphysical plague to those who shared his worldview. They are all in the early stages, feeling the same righteous fury and clarity of purpose.

This reframes the city's conflict entirely. It is not a debate between two political parties. It is a struggle between the city's healthy, uninfected population, represented by the fearful and cautious Elder [[Yrsa]], and a burgeoning nest of sleeper agents who do not even know what they have become. The party, upon their arrival, has just walked into the middle of an epidemic that is about to reach its final, violent stage.
