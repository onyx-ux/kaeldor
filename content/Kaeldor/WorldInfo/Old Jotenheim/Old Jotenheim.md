# Welcome to Jotunheim
To speak of [[Old Jotenheim]] in the modern age is to speak a dead language. It is a name that lingers on the edges of maps, a rumour of a place that chose to be forgotten. While the dwarves of [[Khaz Modan]] met the [[#Great Sundering]] with the ringing of hammers, the giants met it with a profound and absolute silence that has not been broken since.

The journey to their domain is a pilgrimage into stillness. The land itself becomes a barrier; the winds that claw at the passes are not natural, but seem to whisper a single, monotonous command: turn back. The cold is not merely weather; it is a wall, a sentient thing that seeks to freeze the resolve of any who would approach. The snow falls not in flurries, but in heavy, silent curtains that swallow sound and hide the path.

There are no gates to [[Old Jotenheim]], no walls of quarried stone. There is only the ice. What at first appears to be a colossal glacier, a river of frozen blue, reveals itself to be a deliberate construction. Its surface, impossibly sheer, is etched with runes of warding so vast that a single symbol is the size of a human citadel. They hum with a deep and ancient power, a primal magic that makes the teeth ache and the air crackle.

Beyond this glacial wall, one does not find a city, but a graveyard of sleeping gods. Immense structures, monolithic and utterly silent, pierce the ever-present gloom. They are not built, but seem to have been commanded to rise from the bedrock itself. There are no signs of life, nor are there signs of the war that ravaged the rest of the north. There are no scorch marks, no broken battlements. There is only a perfect, chilling stillness. The wind does not stir here. The snow does not fall. Sound itself seems to die before it can echo.

The giants are not seen. Whether they retreated into the heart of the mountain, or lie entombed within the magical ice, or simply faded into memory, none can say. To be here is to feel like a microbe under the gaze of a forgotten power. The city's silence is its only greeting; the crushing scale of its emptiness is its only welcome. To visit [[Old Jotenheim]] is to intrude upon a long-held secret. It is a place that has not fallen, but has simply… stopped. It waits, holding its breath for a generation, or an epoch, for a purpose known only to itself. The greatest mystery of the north is not what happened to the giants, but what they might do when they finally decide to wake.

# Jotunheim (City Story)
## Jotunheim (The Rime-Heart Giants)
[[Old Jotenheim]] was once an ancient, isolated kingdom of mountain giants. Now, it is a silent garrison for the [[Lich King]]'s most powerful slaves, a kingdom conquered from within by a single, devastating act of necromantic plague.

* **Leadership:** [[King Ymir]] the Rime-Hearted (Corrupted King of the Giants).
* **Description:** The being once known as [[King Ymir]] is now a horrifying monument to [[Lich King|Valameer]]'s power. He is a colossal giant encased in jagged, blackened ice, his great-axe fused to his hand. A crown of frozen rock is embedded in his skull, and his eyes burn with cold, blue malice. Through a great fissure in his chest, a pulsing shard of magical ice—the original heartstone that infected his entire race—can be seen, binding him and all giants to the [[Lich King]]'s will.
* **Alignment to the Throne:** The Enemy (Enslaved Vassal). [[Old Jotenheim]] is a vassal state of the [[Lich King]]. Its people are not allies but slaves, their minds and souls irrevocably frozen by the [[######The Rime-Heart Plague]]. They serve as [[Lich King|Valameer]]'s living siege engines and elite heavy infantry, their immense strength now guided by a singular, malevolent intelligence. Their alignment is not a choice; it is a symptom of their infection.
