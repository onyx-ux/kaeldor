# Welcome to Nekros
Welcome to [[Nekros]], the city that remembers too much. It was not always a place of fear. In the hopeful years of the Age of Harmony, [[Nekros]] was [[King Illhoof]]'s most profound and compassionate experiment. It was built upon a unique confluence of spiritual energy, a place where the veil between worlds was thin, and the souls of the fallen were naturally drawn to rest. This was the City of Remembrance, a sanctuary of soft mists and silent gardens where the living could walk, for a time, with the peaceful, spectral forms of their departed loved ones. It was a city of gentle grief, of last goodbyes, a promise that those who were lost were never truly forgotten.

But the promise was broken. When the mysterious [[Lich King]], [[Lich King|Valameer]], first appeared, he did not lay siege to [[Nekros]]—he poisoned its heart. He took the city’s unique spiritual energy, its very reason for being, and twisted it. The gentle spirits were ensnared, their memories weaponized, their sorrow forged into a source of unending power. The haven became a hell.

To approach [[Nekros]] today is to walk into an apocalyptic burial ground. The elegant mausoleums now stand as jagged, crumbling teeth against a sky of bruised purple. The quiet reflection pools are now stagnant pits of ethereal sludge from which malformed, shrieking things crawl forth. The peaceful, wandering spirits have been corrupted into mournful shades, their spectral forms contorted with agony, or worse, pressed into the ranks of the [[Lich King]]'s armies as unfeeling soldiers.

And at the centre of the city, where once stood the great Terrace of Tranquil Memory, now stands a monolithic structure that seems to defy all laws of architecture and reason. It is a spire of jagged, black, crystal-like material that pulses with a sickening inner light. But it is not stone. To look upon it is to witness a constant, silent torrent of spectral forms being pulled from the air and absorbed into its structure. These are the new souls of [[Kaeldor]], the soldiers who die on the borders, the civilians lost to plague or despair—all are drawn here now, not to a place of rest, but as fuel.

The local legends—the terrified whispers from those who have escaped the [[Lich King]]'s grasp—call this structure the [[Heart of Sorrows]]. They say it is not an object of power, but the very engine of the city's corruption. They claim the [[Lich King]] has woven his own life force into the collective grief of every soul tethered to this place. His immortality is not held within a hidden jewel, but in the unending anguish of a thousand stolen memories. To destroy him, one would not need to shatter a phylactery, but somehow grant peace to the tormented souls that have become his anchor to the mortal world.

So, welcome to [[Nekros]], the city that was meant to be a second chance. It is now the [[Lich King]]'s sanctuary, the heart of his revenge, and a place where every memory is a brick in his fortress of undeath. Tread carefully, for the very air you breathe is made of ghosts, and the ground you walk upon is paved with sorrow.

# Valdrak / Nekros (City Story)
## Valdrak / Nekros (The Lich King's Domain)
The leadership in the blighted north is a rigid hierarchy of undeath, with all power flowing directly from its supreme master.

* **Leadership:**
    * [[Lich King|Valameer]], the [[Lich King]]: Supreme Ruler.
    * The [[Scourge Wardens]]: His direct lieutenants.
* **Description:** [[Lich King|Valameer]] is a being of unknown origin and immense power. His physical form, if he has a consistent one, is rarely seen. He rules from the dark fortress of [[Valdrak]], his will projecting across the entire [[The Land of Razuul]]. The [[Scourge Wardens]] are the twisted remnants of the most powerful sentient undead who once lived in [[Nekros]]. They now serve as [[Lich King|Valameer]]'s generals. Notable among them are [[Ghor'Thul the Abomination]], a hulking brute of flesh and bone who leads the shock troops, and [[The Weeping Lady]], a banshee commander whose psychic screams of terror precede the main army.
* **Alignment to the Throne:** The Ultimate Enemy. [[Lich King|Valameer]]'s motivation is unknown, but his actions point towards the complete eradication or subjugation of all life in [[Kaeldor]]. His lieutenants are not allies but extensions of his will, fanatically loyal because their own minds have been erased or irrevocably enslaved by his power.
