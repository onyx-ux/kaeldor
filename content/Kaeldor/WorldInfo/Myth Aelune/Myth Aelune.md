# Welcome to Myth'Aelune
There was a time when to find [[Myth Aelune]], one simply had to walk toward the light. For at the heart of the ancient forest stood the great [[World Tree]], and its leaves wept a silver, dewy luminescence that nourished the land and served as a beacon for all of [[Kaeldor]]. The light is now fading.

To approach [[Myth Aelune]] today is to walk through a forest that holds its breath. The air is still and heavy with an ancient, unspoken sorrow. The paths are overgrown, and the once-joyous whispers of the leaves have been replaced by a dry, rustling sigh. The city reveals itself not with a grand gate, but with the gradual appearance of elegant flets and spiraling staircases woven seamlessly around the boughs of colossal, silver-barked trees. But all of it, all of this breathtaking, natural architecture, exists in the shadow of the heart of the forest: the [[World Tree]].

It is a titan of bark and bough, its highest branches still scraping the clouds, yet it is a titan in its death throes. The silver light of its canopy is now a sickly, pale yellow. Its leaves, once perpetually vibrant, now brown and curl at the edges, falling like tears to the forest floor. And at its base, where the city's main enclave rests, the corruption is plain to see. The great roots, as thick as buildings, are warped and blackened, with pulsing veins of dark violet corruption crawling up the ancient bark like a spider's web. This is the sickness that has poisoned paradise, a creeping blight that has seeped into the soil from the blighted [[The Land of Razuul]].

The Elves of [[Myth Aelune]] are as graceful as the legends say, but their ancient joy has given way to a grim, focused resolve. They move through their withering home like elegant specters, their songs now quiet, somber prayers offered to the dying heart of their world. But prayer is no longer their only answer. This is a city arming itself. In the sacred groves, the long-limbed Elven sentinels, their faces grim masks of determination, practice with bows as tall as men. On balconies overlooking the sickened roots, ancient bladesmiths, who had not forged a weapon of war in a thousand years, now sharpen elegant leaf-bladed swords that hum with a faint, angry light. They are a people preparing for a different kind of war—not for territory or for grudges, but a spiritual battle for the very life of [[Kaeldor]] itself.

To be welcomed in [[Myth Aelune]] is to be a witness to a quiet, desperate vigil. You are an interruption in a city-wide prayer, a stranger in a house that is slowly dying. The Elves will greet you not with hostility, but with a deep, sorrowful weariness. They look at you with ancient eyes and ask a silent, desperate question: have you come to watch their world die, or have you come to help them save it?

# Myth'Aelune (City Story)
## Myth'Aelune (The Elven Enclave)
Like the dwarves, the elves are a refugee population, though they have responded to their isolation with melancholy reflection rather than simmering rage.

* **Leadership:** [[Loremistress Lyraelle]].
* **Description:** [[Lyraelle]] is ancient, and her movements are slow and deliberate, as if weighed down by immense, unseen grief. She leads the small elven community in preserving the songs, history, and culture of the starlit forests they will never see again. She is serene, but her wisdom is tinged with a profound, unshakable sorrow.
* **Alignment to the Throne:** Neutrally Loyal. [[Lyraelle]] understands why the [[#Great Sundering]] was necessary and holds no ill will towards [[King Illhoof]]. However, she and her people see themselves as guests in a human city, not subjects of a human king. They will defend [[Aeris Gard]] as their last sanctuary, but they remain aloof from political struggles, viewing the dynastic conflicts of "short-lived" races as fleeting.
