## The Rime-Heart Plague
The corruption of [[Old Jotenheim]] was not a conquest of armies, but a single, insidious act of sabotage that unfolded in the shadow of [[Kaeldor]]'s greatest cataclysm. As the [[Lich King]] [[Lich King|Valameer]]'s hordes crashed against the north, he dispatched his most cunning agent, [[Oswin|Dread-Revenant Oswin]], on a mission of absolute priority.

## Infiltration During the Sundering
While the [[1. Veil Warden|Veil Warden]] channeled their power to lift [[Aeris Gard]] and the [[1. Order of the Adamant Veil]] braced for the war's final, desperate chapter, [[Oswin]] was already within the capital's walls. Cloaked in powerful illusion magic, he had slipped in amongst the flood of northern refugees weeks prior. During the [[#Great Sundering]]'s violent, world-breaking chaos, all eyes were on the sky above or the enemy beyond. No one was watching for a lone figure moving against the tide of panic. As the [[Aegis of Aeris]] tore itself from the earth, [[Oswin]] slipped unnoticed through the newly formed, unstable southern peaks and began a patient, solitary trek northward into the frozen heart of the continent.

## The Gift of the Lich King
[[Oswin]] did not fear the sentient cold or the magical wards of [[Old Jotenheim]], for he carried a sliver of his master's power: a pulsating, black heartstone etched with runes of domination. The ancient defenses of the giants were designed to repel armies and overt magical assaults, not to recognize a single supplicant bearing tribute.

When [[Oswin]] reached the colossal ice gates, he did not attempt to breach them. He simply waited. He was eventually met by the giants' king, the mighty [[King Ymir]]. Seeing no army and no threat, only a lone, kneeling undead knight, [[King Ymir]]'s pride became his downfall. [[Oswin]] offered the heartstone not as a weapon, but as a "gift of fealty" from one great northern power to another. He claimed his "King of Silence" wished to honor the true strength of the mountains.

## The Plague of Ice
Intrigued and arrogant, [[King Ymir]] accepted the tribute. He had the heartstone brought to the center of his great throne hall, a chamber carved into the living ice at the core of their mountain home. The moment it was set upon its pedestal, the heartstone unleashed its true purpose.

It did not explode. It emitted a silent, invisible wave of necromantic energy—a magical plague designed specifically for the giants' ancient physiology. The [[#The Rime-Heart Plague]]did not rot flesh; it froze the soul. Starting with [[King Ymir]] and radiating outwards, the giants' free will was encased in ice. Their spirits were shattered and replaced with cold, absolute obedience to [[Lich King|Valameer]]. Their bodies became vessels, their minds wiped clean.

[[King Ymir]] himself became the plague's epicenter. The black heartstone burrowed into his chest, transforming his own heart into a conduit for the [[Lich King]]'s will—the first Rime-Heart. The proud kingdom of [[Old Jotenheim]] did not fall to screams and battle, but to a perfect, chilling silence as its people became the [[Lich King]]'s greatest prize.
