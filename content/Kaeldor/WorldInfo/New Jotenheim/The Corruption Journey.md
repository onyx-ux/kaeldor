# The Corruptions Journey
Before the [[#Great Sundering]], the rolling highlands north of the southern cliffs were known as the Sunken Plains, a place of profound and wild beauty. It was a sea of windswept grass, dotted with ancient, solitary oaks and carpeted in vast swathes of star-lilies and mountain bells.

Great herds of shaggy crag-walkers moved like living hills across the landscape, their calls echoing in the clear, crisp air. Crystal-clear streams, born from the northern peaks, carved their way through the valleys, their waters so pure that the stones at the bottom seemed to magnify in the sun. It was a land vibrant with colour, sound, and life.

Then, [[Oswin|Dread-Revenant Oswin]] passed through it.
He did not come with an army, nor did he burn the plains with dragon-fire. The corruption he brought was a quiet, creeping pestilence, a personal aura of anti-life that leeched the soul from the land itself. His journey was a patient, relentless march northward, and he left a permanent scar upon the world.

Where his armoured sabatons trod, the living grass did not burn, but instantly died. It turned a brittle, spectral grey, each blade flash-frozen with a rime that did not melt under the noon sun. The very soil beneath his feet became sterile, turning to lifeless dust. In his wake, he left a trail—a perfectly delineated path of grey decay that cut a dead, straight line through the rolling green hills, an unnatural wound that would never heal.

But the pollution was not confined to his path. An aura of absolute cold and silence moved with him, a bubble of necromantic energy that drained the life from everything nearby.

The vibrant fields of wildflowers he passed did not merely wilt; their colours seemed to run from them, bleeding into the air until the petals were left ashen black before crumbling into nothing. The ancient oaks grew gaunt as he approached, their leaves falling in a single, silent cascade, leaving behind skeletal branches that looked like grasping, bony fingers. The fauna of the plains fled from him in a silent, unnatural terror. The herds of crag-walkers, who would stand their ground against any living predator, broke and stampeded away from the silent, walking dread. Those smaller creatures that could not flee fast enough simply ceased. Flocks of birds fell silent in the sky and dropped like stones, their bodies unmarked but utterly devoid of life.

When [[Oswin]] forded the crystal streams, the water grew sluggish and dark in his presence. A thin, oily shadow seemed to coat the surface, and the smooth river stones became coated in a slick, grey algae. The water that flowed from his path downstream was tainted, carrying the subtle poison of the grave with it. He brought a plague of silence. The chirping of insects, the rustle of leaves, the song of birds—all of it died within a hundred paces of him. He moved through a world that held its breath in terror, a dissonant note of absolute death in a symphony of life.

By the time [[Oswin|Dread-Revenant Oswin]] had crossed the Sunken Plains and reached the foothills of the northern peaks, the land behind him was a ghost of itself. It was a perfect landscape drained of all colour, sound, and warmth. It was a masterpiece of nature that had been meticulously, silently un-painted. The dead, grey scar of his journey remained, pointing like a bone-white arrow directly towards the silent peaks of [[Old Jotenheim]], a testament to the fact that the most profound horrors do not always arrive with a scream, but sometimes with a whisper of absolute cold.
