In an age now remembered only in song, the continent of [[Kaeldor]] was a symphony of life. From the echoing mountain halls of [[Khaz Modan]] to the starlit forests of [[Myth Aelune]], all races found their place in the world's great design under the benevolent rule of [[King Illhoof]] Fenmir. From his gleaming capital of [[Aeris Gard]], he championed an era of unprecedented peace, his most audacious act being the creation of a sanctuary for the sentient undead. In the desolate lands beyond the [[Ash-Veil Peaks]], he founded [[The Land of Razuul]] and the city of [[Nekros]]—a haven for minds that had outlasted their mortal shells. For a time, the King's Great Experiment flourished.

But the seeds of ruin were sown not in the north, but within the royal court itself. King [[King Odegar]] Fenmir, a man who believed strength was forged in strife, pitted his two sons against each other. [[King Illhoof|Illhoof]], the younger, was blessed with charisma, while the elder, [[Lich King|Eldhoof]], was perpetually eclipsed, turning his ambition to the forbidden arcane arts. The schism became absolute on [[Lich King|Eldhoof]]’s eighteenth birthday when King [[King Odegar]] proclaimed the younger [[King Illhoof|Illhoof]] as his heir. Consumed by a lifetime of rage, [[Lich King|Eldhoof]] stormed from the palace in a fury and vanished into the night. He was never seen again, presumed by the court to have thrown himself from the cliffs or to be living in self-imposed exile, his name becoming a sorrowful footnote in the history of the Fenmir dynasty.

# Great Sundering

This desperate command was born from the ultimate devastation—the fall of [[Valdrak]]. The great northern bastion, the final shield against the darkness, had been breached and its populace consumed, its very stones now crawling with the [[Lich King]]'s corruption. With the horrific loss of [[Valdrak]] sealing the north's fate, [[King Illhoof]] commanded the [[1. Veil Warden|Veil Warden]] to enact the [[Great Sundering]]. In a ritual that shook the continent, they lifted the capital of [[Aeris Gard]] into the sky. In the gaping wound left behind, the land buckled and surged upwards, creating a new, impassable ring of mountains. This circular range, known today as the [[Aegis of Aeris]], now stands as the kingdom's ultimate border wall a tombstone for a lost city and a quarantine against the horrors it now held.

With this cataclysm, [[Kaeldor]] entered a long, grim twilight. [[King Illhoof]]’s spirit fractured, his dream of harmony replaced by grim pragmatism. He ruled a broken, isolated land, haunted by the specter of the [[Lich King]] and the ghost of his long-lost brother.

Now, the respite ends. Shambling figures are seen in the foothills of the sundered mountains, their armor bearing a dark, twisted mockery of an old royal crest. The whispers of the [[Lich King]] have returned. [[King Illhoof]], aged by a war he does not understand against an enemy with no known past, calls for heroes. For a new war brews, one that may unravel not only the future of [[Kaeldor]].
