# Welcome to Aeris Gard
To the desperate souls on the ground, it is a miracle. A star that never falls, a promise of divinity, a silent, beautiful testament to a peace that was stolen from the world. It is the last glimmer of the Age of Harmony, a paradise held aloft by magic and memory. But to the few who ascend, the truth reveals itself. Welcome to [[Aeris Gard]], the gilded cage.

The journey upward, whether by the beating wings of a royal griffon or the shimmering tear of a teleportation circle, is a journey away from reality. You leave behind the grit and grime of [[Stonebridge]], a city that chokes on its nightly fog and shivers through its "[[The Hour of Silence]]." You ascend past the smells of mud and fear and enter the crisp, clean air of the heavens. The city is, by all accounts, perfect. Gleaming white spires seem to scratch the belly of the sky, linked by bridges of solid, unwavering light. Magically-fed gardens bloom in eternal spring, their beauty a stark, cruel contrast to the blighted lands of the north. The city is populated by a nobility wrapped in silks and jewels, their lives a perpetual gala of blissful denial.

But this perfection is a meticulously crafted lie. The city's eternal light is paid for by the darkness below. Its serene quiet is bought with the screams of the north that were ignored decades ago. Every citizen of [[Aeris Gard]] lives with the subtle, gnawing poison of their own survival. This fragile peace is kept not by diplomats, but by two orders born from the [[#Great Sundering]]. The first are the [[1. Veil Warden|Veil Warden]], the city's architects and jailers. They are robed, silent figures who glide through the plazas, their unseen eyes constantly monitoring the arcane energies that keep the city afloat and the world at bay. They are the keepers of the King's terrible secret. The second is their sword hand, the [[1. Order of the Adamant Veil]]. Their knights are breathtaking figures, their silver-etched armor polished to a mirror sheen, reflecting the city's perfect sky. They are the beautiful, flawless soldiers sent to purge the creeping horrors from [[Stonebridge]], a grim reminder that the rot they fled still seeps from the ground below.

To be in [[Aeris Gard]] is to be haunted by ghosts. It is to feel the bitter silence from the halls of [[Khaz Modan]], whose dwarves see this city as a monument to their betrayal. It is to sense the deafening stillness of the giants of [[Old Jotenheim]], who chose to vanish rather than witness this broken world. It is to know that the orcs of [[Khar-Thul]] have turned their backs completely, finding new gods in their own sky rather than serve a king in a floating castle.

And at the very heart of this magnificent lie, in the tallest spire where the air is thin and cold, sits the Sundered King. [[King Illhoof]], aged not by years but by a single, impossible choice, rules from a throne of sorrow. He is haunted by two ghosts: the brother who vanished into the night decades ago, and the [[Lich King]] whose appearance shortly after feels like a twisted act of cosmic irony. So, welcome to [[Aeris Gard]], the soul of the Kingdom of Fenmir. A paradise built on a foundation of screams. A fortress of privilege pretending to be a beacon of hope. A beautiful, perfect, and deeply broken lie, waiting for the day the ground remembers what it has lost.

# Aeris Gard (City Story)
## Aeris Gard (The Flying Capital)
As the last bastion of the living, [[Aeris Gard]]'s leadership is a tense coalition of military, arcane, and civil authorities, all operating under the ultimate rule of the King.

* **Leadership:**
    * [[King Illhoof]] Fenmir: The supreme ruler.
    * [[Lord-General Marius]]: Commander of the [[1. Order of the Adamant Veil]].
    * [[Magister Kaelan]]: Acting leader of the [[1. Veil Warden|Veil Warden]].
    * High Steward [[Elara Vance]]: Civil Administrator of the city.
* **Description:**
    * [[King Illhoof]] is a man fractured by grief. He is pragmatic and grim, making the hard decisions needed for survival, but the fire of his youth and his dream of harmony are gone, replaced by the heavy weight of his crown.
    * [[Lord-General Marius]] is a veteran of the war against [[Lich King|Valameer]], his face a roadmap of old scars and weariness. He is fiercely competent, tactically brilliant, and carries the sorrow of having witnessed the fall of the north firsthand.
    * [[Magister Kaelan]] ([[Blake]]'s tutor) is the highest-ranking member of the mage order that enacted the [[#Great Sundering]]. He is disciplined, severe, and views magic as a necessary, dangerous tool for survival.
    * High Steward [[Vance]] is a sharp, non-noble woman who rose through merit. She manages the city's logistics—food, water, infrastructure—and is constantly fighting for resources for the common folk.
* **Alignment to the Throne:**
    * [[Marius]] is Unyieldingly Loyal. He serves the King without question, believing a strong, unified command is the only thing preventing their extinction.
    * [[Kaelan]] is Pragmatically Loyal. His loyalty is to the survival of [[Aeris Gard]] itself. He serves the King as long as he believes the King's decisions are sound, but would take matters into his own hands if he felt the throne was leading them to ruin.
    * [[Vance]] is Loyal to the People. She respects the throne as the symbol of order but will fiercely oppose the King or any noble if their actions threaten the welfare of the city's populace.
