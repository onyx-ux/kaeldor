# The Royal Lineage of House Fenmir
## First Generation: The Founding
* **[[King Henry]] I Fenmir** - "The Unifier"
* m. **Queen Astrid**
* **Reign:** United the warring human tribes of [[Kaeldor]] and established the Kingdom, laying the foundation for the Fenmir Dynasty.

## Second Generation: The Age of Law
* **[[King Alaric]] Fenmir** - "The Just" (Son of Henry I)
* m. **Queen Elara**
* **Reign:** A scholarly king who established the formal legal codes of the kingdom, renowned for his fairness and the establishment of a peaceful judiciary.

## Third Generation: The Age of Prosperity
* **[[King Gareth]] Fenmir** - "The Builder" (Son of Alaric)
* m. **Queen Genevieve**
* **Reign:** Commissioned and oversaw the construction of the gleaming capital, [[Aeris Gard]]. His reign marked the beginning of an unprecedented era of peace and prosperity.

## Fourth Generation: The Age of Faith
* **[[Oswin|King Oswin]] Fenmir** - "The Pious" (Son of Gareth)
* m. **Queen Isolde**
* **Reign:** A deeply religious king whose reign was marked by the construction of grand cathedrals and the strengthening of religious orders throughout [[Kaeldor]].

## Fifth Generation: The First Cracks
* **[[King Torsten]] Fenmir** - "The Worried" (Son of Oswin)
* m. **Queen Seraphina**
* **Reign:** A troubled reign where the first whispers of darkness and unrest from the far north began to surface, leading to a more isolationist and defensive posture.

## Sixth Generation: The Seeds of Ruin
* **[[King Odegar]] Fenmir** (Son of Torsten)
* m. **Queen Lyra**
* **Reign:** A harsh and cruel king who believed strength was forged only in strife. His defining legacy was instigating the bitter rivalry between his two sons.
* **Children of Odegar and Lyra:**
    * **Prince [[Lich King|Eldhoof]] Fenmir** (Firstborn Son)
    * **Status:** Whereabouts Unknown. Vanished after being passed over for the throne. The inciting figure of the "Fenmir Schism."
    * **[[King Illhoof]] Fenmir** (Second Son)
    * The current King of [[Kaeldor]].

## Seventh Generation: The Sundered Kingdom
* **[[King Illhoof]] Fenmir** - "The Sundered King"
* m. **Queen [[Annelise]]** (Deceased)
    * Died during childbirth, a tragedy that compounded the King's grief after the [[#Great Sundering]] and sparked a succession crisis.
* **Children of Illhoof and Annelise:**
    * **[[Princess Anya]] Fenmir** (Firstborn Child, Age 19)
    * The public face of the royal line; unable to inherit the throne by law.
    * **[[Theron]] Fenmir** (Age 13)
    * His death created the succession crisis that led to [[Blake]]'s reveal.
    * **Prince [[Blake]] Fenmir** (Second Son, Age 13)
    * The secret heir, raised in isolation and recently revealed to the court.
