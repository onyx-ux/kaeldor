# 🐲 Tyris
## Name: Tyris
* **Race:** Tiefling - Bloodline of Mephistopheles
* **Class:** Draconic Bloodline Sorcerer

## Tyris's Journey
[[Tyris]] grew up knowing his mother, [[Namine]], as his only relative. [[Namine]], the youngest daughter of the [[Mephiskir]] family, had [[Tyris]] with a "human," an act deemed unacceptable by her family. Unbeknownst to them and [[Tyris]], his father was no ordinary human, but the great red dragon [[Darmok]] in human form. [[Namine]] kept this secret from both [[Tyris]] and [[Darmok]] to protect her son. Thankfully, [[Namine]] found a community of tieflings in [[Valdrak]] who welcomed her and [[Tyris]].

### Early Life and Unstable Magic
As a child, the mix of [[Tyris]]'s devil and dragon fire magic became unstable, causing every tantrum to turn into a fiery mess. His mother, with her knowledge of magic, could mostly keep it in check. However, as [[Tyris]] grew, his magic became more powerful and unstable, to the point where [[Namine]] could no longer contain it. As his fires grew larger and more explosive, [[Namine]]'s worry deepened.

### The Incident in Valdrak
Growing up in [[Valdrak]], [[Tyris]] met a diverse group of people, but he bonded best with a bronze dragonborn boy named [[Macaan]]. When they were both around 15, they had a fight over something [[Tyris]] can't even remember. The next thing he knew, [[Macaan]]’s house was on fire, and everyone was frantically trying to put it out. No one was hurt, but the damage was done. [[Tyris]] felt he had no choice but to leave or risk hurting the people he cared about. Knowing his mother would try to stop him, he wrote a tearful note and left for [[Aeris Gard]], taking a small pendant his mother said was from his father and a small amount of gold he had saved.

### On the Road to Aeris Gard
Along the way, [[Tyris]] met [[Granor]], an old traveling merchant who offered him a ride on his cart in exchange for help with heavy lifting. [[Granor]] sensed [[Tyris]] didn't want to talk much and mostly left him alone, occasionally teaching him about surviving on the road. When they reached [[Aeris Gard]], [[Tyris]] was struck by the city's beauty but could tell he wasn't welcome. He decided to head to [[Stonebridge]] instead. Before he left, [[Granor]] bid him farewell and gifted him some money and supplies.

## Life in Stonebridge
In [[Stonebridge]], [[Tyris]] moved from tavern to tavern, taking odd jobs to earn his keep. He never stayed in one place too long, worried he would get too attached and his emotions would get the best of him – or because they already had. After about a year and a few small fires, he began to notice his vague description on wanted boards. He decided it would be best to leave the city and go to the farms on the outskirts.

### A New Home and Growing Fears
After a few months of working on farms, [[Tyris]] finally settled in a village not patrolled by guards, with a family who offered him a spare cottage on their farm. They welcomed him despite his attempts to remain distant. [[Tyris]] and one of their sons, [[Hal]], became quick friends while working the farm together. As he grew closer to the family, his worry about hurting them intensified, especially when [[Hal]] discovered his magic.

### The Vision and the Flight
The stress culminated one night when [[Tyris]] had a vision: the family house engulfed in flames, then [[Hal]] being arrested by guards, and finally the rest of the town chasing him down. [[Tyris]] woke up screaming, covered in sweat, the cottage around him consumed by flames. He stumbled out of bed, the night sky lit by the huge fire, and saw a nearby field beginning to catch as well. He ran to the main house, waking the family, who then ran to sound the alarms and warn the rest of the village. As chaos erupted with people trying to put out the fire, [[Tyris]] began to run, sharing one final somber look with [[Hal]] as he disappeared into the fields.

## Journey to Myth'Aelune
For the next few weeks, [[Tyris]] wandered east, using the survival skills he learned from [[Granor]], stopping in towns for only a day or two. At some point, seeing the [[World Tree]] in the distance and remembering his mother's stories of the elves, he hoped someone there could help him. He decided to head to [[Myth Aelune]]. Upon arrival, [[Tyris]] was awed by the size of the [[World Tree]] and the ancient elven architecture, but he could tell something was off; it wasn't like the stories his mother had told him.

### Seeking Help from Erfir Elmsrest
After a few weeks of asking around, someone suggested he speak to [[Erfir Elmsrest]]. Walking up to Erfir’s house, [[Tyris]] felt the hair on the back of his neck stand up, but he pushed through his nerves, knowing he had nowhere else to go. He knocked on the door. [[Erfir Elmsrest|Erfir]] was an old, caring elf who admitted he didn't know what was causing [[Tyris]]'s magic to be so unstable, but he offered to help him control his emotions better, which [[Tyris]] accepted.

### Training and a Fateful Meeting
For the next six months, [[Tyris]] stayed with [[Erfir Elmsrest|Erfir]], learning to control his emotions through meditation and breathing techniques. There were good days and bad days, but he was mostly holding back the fire.

That was until a messenger from the [[Loremistress Lyraelle]] summoned [[Erfir Elmsrest|Erfir]] for an urgent meeting. [[Tyris]] was allowed to go with [[Erfir Elmsrest|Erfir]] but had to wait outside. As he sat, he heard a few raised voices for what felt like the first time in [[Myth Aelune]]. He caught only a few words here and there: something about [[Lich King|Valameer]], a lich, then [[King Illhoof]]. When he heard "[[Valdrak]]," his ears perked up. [[Tyris]] started to piece things together, then heard, "He left them all for dead!" It clicked.

### Uncontrolled Rage and Banishment
All [[Tyris]] could feel was rage building inside him. He grew hotter and hotter, desperately trying to hold it all back, but he couldn't shake the thought that he had left for nothing, that it was all for nothing. At this point, the other people waiting outside the meeting began to notice him. The room filled with overwhelming heat. Flames flowed all around [[Tyris]], then as he screamed, completely losing control, everything went black.

## A New Path in Khaz Modan
As [[Tyris]] came to, he saw a 7-foot-tall plague doctor who introduced himself as [[Visage]], leading the cart he lay on. He held his head and asked what happened. [[Visage]] handed him a note from [[Erfir Elmsrest|Erfir]] that reminded him of his breathing techniques, then described what had happened to [[Valdrak]] and [[Aeris Gard]]. The note explained that because he nearly blew up the [[World Tree]] and a meeting of very important people, he was to be imprisoned and silenced, but [[Erfir Elmsrest|Erfir]] had talked them down to banishment. The note went on to tell him about a blacksmith friend of [[Erfir Elmsrest|Erfir]] who could use his fire.

When they reached [[Khaz Modan]], [[Tyris]] thanked [[Visage]] then went to [[Ranrum]] the blacksmith, who took him to his forge and explained the job. The work was simple: when Ran called for it, blast as much fire as possible. It was a good outlet for his emotions, plus the ale helped, as weeks merged into months and seasons blended together. But as his anger subsided and burns developed on his hands, [[Tyris]] and [[Ranrum]] knew that at some point, he would have to leave. So, with a big dwarven hug, Ran bid him farewell as [[Tyris]] ventured back out into the world.
