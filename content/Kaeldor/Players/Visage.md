# 🦸 Visage
## Name: Visage
* **Class:** Way of Mercy Monk
* **Race:** Aasimar

## Arrival in Khar-Thul
In [[Khar-Thul]], where gods are stone and the sky hums with prophecy, [[Visage]] fell like an omen. The night he arrived, the smog above the eastern cliffs split with a flash of radiance and fire. Witnesses claimed a burning star plummeted from the heavens, crashing into the mud slicked plaza beneath the monoliths. When the smoke cleared, they found not a crater but a child. Pale skinned, amber eyed, and eerily silent. [[Visage]].

The goblins were the first to gather, drawn more by curiosity than reverence. The orcs, slower to trust, hung back and watched. Only one stepped forward. [[Shmick]], a soot stained goblin alchemist with burn marks on both hands and more heart than caution. He took the boy in without question. In a city where usefulness was worship, he saw potential not in what [[Visage]] was, but in what he could become.

### Shmick’s Alchemy Shop and Early Upbringing
[[Shmick]]’s shop was more than a business. It was a vital piece of [[Khar-Thul]]’s living machinery. Nestled between smithies and scrap yards, it catered to the city's laborers, tinkerers, and bruisers. Broken limbs from collapsing lifts, burns from misfired forges, chemical scalds from failed experiments, [[Shmick]] treated them all. His shelves were lined with jars of coagulants, salves brewed from mushroom spores, regenerative syrups, and tonics so potent they rattled the bones.

This is where [[Visage]] grew up. Among the hiss of bubbling concoctions and the groans of injured clients, he learned anatomy before language and triage before arithmetic. [[Shmick]] hired wandering monks, hedge healers, and retired battlefield medics to educate him. Some taught technique. Others taught restraint. All were handpicked to reinforce the same lesson: help those who need it, not those who can afford it. “Pain doesn’t wait for coin to show,” [[Shmick]] often said. “And neither should you.”

[[Visage]] absorbed it all. The philosophy. The urgency. The practical knowledge of how to keep a body stitched together in a world constantly trying to tear it apart. He learned to heal with calm precision and to fight with measured grace. His radiant nature, though dimmed by some forgotten fall, manifested as warmth through his hands rather than flame from his fists.

### Defending the Shop and Departure
His turning point came one firelit evening when a gang of scavenger-bandits, high on stolen tinctures, broke into [[Shmick]]’s alchemy store. They underestimated the quiet healer. [[Visage]] met them without hesitation, fists glowing with radiant light, every movement guided by years of training. He dropped them one by one, never with cruelty, only efficiency. When it was over, he stood in the ruins of shattered vials and overturned tables, calmly tending to the wounds he'd just inflicted. [[Shmick]] watched in silence. “You don’t belong to just this place, [[Visage]]. You’re for the ones the world gave up on. The broken ones. The forgotten ones."

So [[Visage]] left [[Khar-Thul]] with a satchel full of volatile tonics, a few trusted tools for alchemy and herbology, and the worn robes of his earliest teacher. He walks the land now not as a soldier, but as a surgeon of mercy. He seeks no throne or treasure, only knowledge of the strange stones above his birthplace, and of whether a fallen soul can bring others back into the light. He fights when he must, heals when he can, and helps without asking for coin. His hands are both weapon and balm. And though the gods may have cast him down, the people he aids raise him high, a whisper in the wind from town to town. A folk hero in motion. A visage of mercy.

### 👥 People of Note
* **[[Nella Rootkind]]**: A halfling herbalist who once traded her family heirloom to save a stranger's life. She has a sharp tongue, a keen sense for plants, and occasional bursts of druidic magic she doesn’t fully understand. She travels with [[Visage]] on occasion, offering practical healing, banter, and wild tea blends.
* **[[Brother Halven]]**: A retired Way of Mercy monk turned pacifist who once mentored [[Visage]]. Halven taught restraint and how to mend spirit as well as flesh. He left [[Visage]] with techniques and morals that were reinforced by [[Shmick]].
* **[[Mernak Willowshade]]**: A half-elf druid and forest warden whom [[Visage]] aided during a blight caused by roaming undead. Though aloof and mistrustful of cities, Mernak occasionally sends word of arcane disturbances through ravens, helping guide [[Visage]] toward sickened lands.
* **[[Rusk the Black-Wire]]**: A goblin artificer from [[Khar-Thul]] who once considered [[Visage]] his rival for [[Shmick]]’s attention. Though competitive, Rusk has begrudgingly supplied [[Visage]] with custom tools and volatile healing stimulants (often with side effects). They exchange notes, insults, and occasionally trade favors when near each other's routes.
