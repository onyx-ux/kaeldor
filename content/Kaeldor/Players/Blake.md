 # 👑 Blake Fenmir
## Name: Blake Fenmir
* **Class:** Shadow Magic Sorcerer
* **Race:** Scourge Aasimar

## Backstory
Spent most of his life in the Royal Palace. Had various Tutors who would visit him in his room to teach him Politics, History, Math, Magic and various other topics.

Was kept a secret for most of his life until he was brought into the light as talks of an heir were brought up by court adviser and the death of his newborn baby boy. The Court would not allow a girl to ascend the throne and many nobles began to scheme behind the scenes. With Talks of Marriage of the kings daughters to Noble Sons or even some schemes of hostile takeover in the shadows.

To combat this "The King" announced that he had a son already and that he is the heir to the throne, this was enough to quiet most of the Noble Unrest, but there was a small group of Nobles who would continue to scheme in the background. ([[Blake]] was 10)

During this Reveal the Tutoring stepped up 10 fold and [[Blake]] was allow to roam the Palace now and the only time he could see the outside world would be when he was announced as the heir, and was pushed back into his room for more study and Swordsmanship. (for 2 years)

Now [[Blake]] being 13 wished to escape and see the world and using the magic he has learned, to escape. And here he is now.

## Session 0.5 - The Prince's Transgression
The event that connects [[Blake]] to his infamous great-uncle, [[Lich King|Eldhoof]], is a deeply forbidden act of rebellion. In the royal library, behind a triple-locked door marked with the old seal of [[King Odegar]], lies the sealed study of Prince [[Lich King|Eldhoof]]. After [[Lich King|Eldhoof]]'s furious departure and disappearance decades ago, his rooms were declared taboo, a monument to a traitor's ambition, and left to gather dust.

Driven by a desire to understand the origins of his family's sorrow, [[Blake]], now 13, uses the subtle magic taught by [[Kaelan]] to bypass the ancient locks and wards. He slips into the forgotten study. It is not a place of sad remembrance, but a frozen tableau of fury. Books on forbidden soul-magic and cosmic entities are thrown about, maps of the continent are covered in aggressive, angry scrawls, and research notes detail [[Lich King|Eldhoof]]'s consuming jealousy of his younger brother. Here, [[Blake]] finds what he seeks: [[Lich King|Eldhoof]]'s personal journal, left open on the desk.

The final entries are not a suicide note, as the official history implies. They are a chronicle of rage and resolve. The last page contains a chilling passage:
“Father and brother think me beaten. They think the ravings of a court soothsayer about a ‘silent king from the north’ are of more import than my birthright. Fools. They mistake my interest in the arcane for madness, when it is truly the path to absolute power. I will not die in this city of fools. I will seek out this supposed silence. I will find this nascent power in the desolate lands, and I will master it. When I return, it will not be as a prince, but as a god. [[Kaeldor]] will know the rule of its true king.”

[[Blake]] does not find answers to the [[Lich King]]'s identity, but he discovers a far more terrifying truth: his great-uncle, the dynasty's great shame, did not die. He left, seeking the very power that would later emerge as their greatest enemy. [[Blake]] takes the journal, now burdened with a terrible secret that re-contextualizes the entire war. He is the only person alive who knows that the shadow that haunts [[Kaeldor]] may have been born from a Fenmir.
