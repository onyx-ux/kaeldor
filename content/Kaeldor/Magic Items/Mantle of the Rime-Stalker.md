
_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_

What at first appears to be a thick, heavy cloak of flawless, white giant-elk hide is revealed to be spun from something far colder. It is woven from solidified frost and captured winter wind, and it does not absorb warmth. It is perpetually cold to the touch and moves with the deceptive silence of falling snow.

**_Lore:_** Not all giants were lumbering behemoths. The Rime-Stalkers were a caste of giant hunters and scouts who moved through the blizzard-swept peaks with uncanny stealth. This mantle was their tool, allowing them to blend into the snow and ice, becoming one with the killing frost. Its magic is that of the avalanche a silent, creeping presence that becomes overwhelming power in an instant.

**_Mechanics:_** Your Constitution score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, you are unaffected by difficult terrain caused by ice or snow.
