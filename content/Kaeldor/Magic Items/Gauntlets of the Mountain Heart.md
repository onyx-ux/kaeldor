_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_

These are massive gauntlets of black, pitted iron, with thick plates covering the fingers and knuckles. A single, powerful rune for "Strength" (Styrkr) is etched into the back of each hand, glowing with a faint, cold blue light. When attuned, they shrink to fit the wearer, though they retain a sense of immense weight and density.

**_Lore:_** The libraries of [[Old Jotenheim]] were not written with ink and quill, but with hammer and chisel on tablets of mountain granite and glacial ice. These gauntlets were worn by the giant Rune-Scribes, whose task was to carve the sagas of their people. They are imbued with the strength needed not only to shape stone, but to break the world if necessary.

**_Mechanics:_** Your Strength score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, you have advantage on Strength checks made to break an inanimate object.
