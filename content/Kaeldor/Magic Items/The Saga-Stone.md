
_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_

A smooth, palm-sized river stone, dark and cold to the touch. A single, intricate rune is carved deeply into its surface, glowing with a faint inner light when held by a creature with a story to tell. The stone feels heavy, not with physical weight, but with the gravity of unspoken histories.

**_Lore:_** The giant Skalds believed a story told with true passion could be coaxed into stone. They would carry these "Saga-Stones" as focusing tools fragments of the great glacial library itself. Holding one allows the bearer to feel the weight and emotion of the tale etched within, lending their own voice a profound, epic quality. This particular stone resonates with the "Lay of the First Jarl," a saga about uniting warring clans under a single banner, not through force, but through shared purpose and conviction.

**_Mechanics:_** Your Charisma score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, you have advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks.
