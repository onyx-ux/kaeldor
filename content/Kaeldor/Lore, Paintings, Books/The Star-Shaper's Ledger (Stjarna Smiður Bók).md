[[Tyris]] has inspected.

**Appearance:** Bound in what appears to be frozen dragonhide, this enormous book is chained to its shelf with links of tarnished, rune-etched silver. The cover is featureless save for a single, deeply inscribed constellation—one that is not visible in the current night sky of [[Kaeldor]]. The pages are vellum, stiff with frost, and the ink shimmers with a faint, silvery light, as if mixed with stardust. Opening it requires breaking a seal of magical ice that reforms after an hour (requiring a successful DC 15 Strength check or dealing at least 10 fire damage to the seal).

**Lore Fragment:** This book appears to be a star-chart and celestial log, detailing the movements of heavenly bodies. However, one passage is circled repeatedly, annotated with frantic, jagged runes.

> _"...the Wanderer returns. The Empty Star. It follows its ancient path, a hole in the heavens that drinks the light. When it last eclipsed the sun, the Crownless Voice walked among us, and the earth was split asunder. The sky-weavers prophesied its return in the Age of Echoes, and that its coming would herald the final silence—not of our choosing, but one of ashes and frost._
> 
> _The star is not a star. It is a prison. And we have heard its prisoner stirring in the deep dark between worlds. Its whispers ride the solar winds, and our corrupted kin have learned to listen. They turn their faces to the sky, not to watch, but to await their master's command."_

This ledger introduces a cosmic element to the threat. [[Lich King|Eldhoof]]'s power may not be his own, but linked to a powerful, imprisoned entity. This provides a seed for a future, far greater threat and re-contextualizes the [[Lich King]] as a harbinger rather than the ultimate source of the corruption.
