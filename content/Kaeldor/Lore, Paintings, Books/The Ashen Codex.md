**Appearance:** This tome is a perversion of the first. Its covers are petrified, blackened bark, cracked and brittle, that flakes away into grey dust at the slightest touch. It is bound with rusted iron chains that seem to writhe with captured shadows. The pages are dry, warped slabs of wood, smelling of old graves and cold ash. The runes within are not written, but _burned_ into the wood, glowing with the familiar, sickening violet light of the [[Lich King]]'s magic. The book radiates a palpable aura of despair and decay.

**The Vision Within:** This codex contains a dark prophecy, a vision of what happens if the heroes fail.

> _"Behold the Carrion Tree. The seed of the world, meant to be a font of life, shall be poisoned by the master's hand. Watered with the blood of kings and nourished by the rot of a fallen kingdom, it shall sprout not as a savior, but as a monument to the final silence. Its roots will not heal the land, but invade it, becoming a network of necrotic nerves through which the Un-King's will shall flow._
> 
> _Its branches will not reach for the sun, but will claw at a sky veiled by the Empty Star, dripping black ichor that slays all it touches. The dead shall hang from its boughs like bitter fruit, their bodies animated by its corrupting sap. The Tree will become a throne, a fortress, and a gallows, its heart a pulsating crystal of pure undeath. It will not sing a song of life, but will broadcast the silent, screaming will of its master across all of [[Kaeldor]], ensuring that all who fall will rise again in his service. It will be the victory of the void, given form."_

This tome presents a dystopian future where [[Lich King|Eldhoof]], empowered by the Wanderer, succeeds in subverting the world's ultimate source of life and turns it into his greatest weapon. It is a vision of absolute failure.
