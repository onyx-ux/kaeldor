**Appearance:** This "book" is a collection of massive, disc-shaped stones threaded onto a central iron pillar. Each stone is a foot thick and ten feet in diameter, and the pillar rises thirty feet high. To read it, one must physically rotate the massive stone discs. The edges of the discs are carved into a continuous mural that tells a story when viewed in sequence. The stone is slick with a thin, unbreakable layer of ice that feels strangely warm to the touch.

**Lore Fragment:** The carvings depict the life and fall of the last giant king who actively ruled before the Great Silence.

> _The murals show a benevolent, crowned giant king ruling from a city of impossible grandeur ([[Old Jotenheim]]'s lost capital). He is shown parlaying with ancient elves and dwarves, sharing knowledge. The next panel shows the [[#Great Sundering]], and the arrival of the "Crownless Voice" ([[Lich King|Eldhoof]]). The king is shown arguing with him, refusing his offer of power. The final, and most disturbing, panel depicts the giant king standing alone on a cliff's edge. He willingly carves the rune for "Silence" into his own chest, then casts his crown into the sea far below before turning to stone. The final inscription reads:_
> 
> _"He chose stone over slavery, silence over screams. His crown lies where the mountain meets the maw, a treasure of fools, for its authority is an eternal burden. Let it sleep in the cold deep, for any who wear it will hear the last words of our king: the toll of our failure and the echo of our shame."_

This book provides a direct, tragic history of the giants' decision and hints at a lost artifact—the Giant King's Crown—and a potential location for it. It also establishes the deep sense of shame and failure that haunts the giants, making their isolation a form of self-imposed penance.
