# A Traveler’s Primer to Kaeldor

_A ledger for wayfarers, compiled by the House of Wayfarers._

---

## [[Aeris Gard]] — “The Flying Capital”

Paradise aloft, purchased by grief below; home of bridges of light, eternal gardens, and the quiet machinery of survival under the [[Great Sundering]].

**Rulers & Orders.** [[King Illhoof]] (the Sundered King); the sword-hand [[Order of the Adamant Veil]] under [[Lord-General Marius]]; the arcane custodians [[Veil Warden|Veil Warden]] under [[Magister Kaelan]]; civil life kept breathing by [[Elara Vance]].

**Linked NPCs.** [[Princess Anya|Princess Anya Fenmir]] (public grace, private steel); [[Ser Korbin|Ser Korbin“The Porcelain Knight” Vance]] (Stonebridge’s mirrored terror); [[Ser Joren]] (knight-escort).

**Notes.** The city’s peace rests on the lie that the north’s screams are far away; its guardians descend often to scour the ground of creeping horror.

---

## [[Stonebridge]] — Shadow of the Sky

Anvil-city beneath [[Aeris Gard]]: mud, fog, conscription, and the dreaded [[The Hour of Silence]].

**Linked Orders & NPCs.** Patrols of the [[Order of the Adamant Veil|Order of the Adamant Veil]] purge the “Somber Veil”; sightings of [[Ser Korbin|Ser Korbin Vance]]
during the fogbound hours.

---

## [[Khar-Thul]] — The Free City

A cliff-hung sprawl of sinew, scrap, and smoke; faith fixed on the turning stones of the [[Circle of Khar-Thul]].

**Rulers.** [[Warchief Ghor’ash|Warchief Ghor'ash]] (the Fist) and [[Chief Tinker Fizzlebang|Chief Tinker Fizzlebang]] (the Brain); a pragmatic duumvirate.

**Linked NPCs.** [[Rusk the Black-Wire]] (goblin artificer, rival-ally to a certain monk); city at large is “utterly indifferent” to thrones—only the [[Circle of Khar-Thul]] matters.

---

## [[Khaz Modan]] — The [[Stone-Exiles]]

A people made into a pressure-valve beneath [[Aeris Gard]]; outwardly cooperative, inwardly nursing a granite-deep grudge.

**Rulers.** [[Borin Stonehand|Guildmaster Borin Stonehand]] and the council of the [[The Stone-Exiles]].

**Linked NPCs.** [[Borin Stonehand]]; the wider [[Stonehand]] kin: [[Bruenor Stonehand]], [[Doric Stonehand]], [[Duramir Darkforge]]; court-kitchen contact [[Bramthorn Emberpan]].

---

## [[Myth Aelune]] — The Withering Crown

An elven city in vigil beneath the ailing [[World Tree]]; a people arming for a spiritual war as leaves brown and roots blacken.

**Ruler.** [[Loremistress Lyraelle]], keeper of songs and sorrow.

**Linked NPCs.** [[Erfir Elmsrest]] (elder who shelters the unstable and counsels calm).

---

## [[Nekros]] — City of Remembrance Unmade

Once a sanctuary where the veil ran thin and farewells were gentle; seized by [[Lich King|Valameer]] the [[Lich King]], its soul-light twisted into a forge of grief.

**Linked Stronghold.** [[Valdrak]], the black bastion raised by [[Lich King|Valameer]], whose fall and corruption forced the [[Great Sundering]].

---

## [[Old Jotenheim]] — The Silent Garrison

A kingdom that chose stillness; now a glacial library and barracks of enslaved giants, conquered from within by the [[The Rime-Heart Plague]].

**Ruler.** [[King Ymir]]—a living monument of blackened ice and will not his own.

**Linked NPCs.** [[King Ymir]]

**Linked Lore & Artifacts.** The murals and tomes speak of the [[The Star-Shaper's Ledger (Stjarna Smiður Bók)|Crownless Voice]], the [[The Ashen Codex]], the [[The Star-Shaper's Ledger (Stjarna Smiður Bók)]], and oaths like “Silence is salvation”.

---

## [[Eldhoof's Watch]] — Lighthouse of a Broken Hope

A southern coastal fortress raised by [[King Illhoof]]—not for ships, but as a beacon to call the lost [[Lich King|Eldhoof]] home.

---

## [[Frost-Tide Landing]] — Last Ferry North

Trade-artery to the [[Aegis of Aeris]] and the wastes of [[Old Jotenheim]]; recently scarred by [[Oswin|King Oswin]] whose anti-life aura froze river and road.

**Witnesses & Leads.** [[Sergeant Hestal]] at the docks; merchant [[Grundi]]; ferryman [[Elric]].

# LORE

- [[Great Sundering]] — In answer to the fall of [[Valdrak]] and the rise of the [[Lich King]] [[Lich King|Valameer]], [[King Illhoof]] commanded the [[Veil Warden]] to wrench [[Aeris Gard]] into the sky, leaving the ring of mountains called the [[Aegis of Aeris]] as a scarred border and tombstone for the north.
- [[Aeris Gard]] & its Orders — A shining citadel above, a burdened shadow below: the city’s peace is maintained by the arcane jailers of the [[Veil Warden]] and the knights of the [[Order of the Adamant Veil]], who descend to purge horrors in [[Stonebridge]].
- [[The Royal Lineage of House Fenmir]] & the Schism — [[King Odegar]] set brothers against each other; the elder, [[Lich King|Prince Eldhoof]], vanished and later appears in giant lore as the [[The Star-Shaper's Ledger (Stjarna Smiður Bók)|The Crownless Voice]]. [[King Illhoof|Illhoof]]’s golden age ended with [[Lich King|Valameer]]’s silence from [[Nekros]] and the raising of [[Valdrak]].
- The Fate of [[Valdrak]] — Once the north’s bastion, now a profane engine of undeath; its desecration fueled the Sundering and the dwarven grudge.
- The [[Circle of Khar-Thul]] — Floating monoliths above [[Khar-Thul]] serve as calendar, oracle, and power, reorienting orcs and goblins away from old thrones toward survival and the stones’ “dance.”
- [[Myth Aelune]] & the [[World Tree]] — The Tree withers, its roots blackened by corruption leeching from [[The Land of Razuul]]; the elves, led by [[Loremistress Lyraelle]], keep vigil and ready for a spiritual war to save life itself.
- [[Old Jotenheim]] & the [[The Rime-Heart Plague]] — The giant kingdom “stopped,” its silence held under the enslaved rule of [[King Ymir]], his chest cored by a pulsing heart-shard that binds his kind to [[Lich King|Valameer]].
- Twin Prophecies of the [[World Tree]] — The hopeful [[The Verdant Canticle]] and the dreadful [[The Ashen Codex]] show opposing futures; the Codex foretells the [[Carrion Tree]], a throne of undeath under the [[The Wanderer, The Empty Star, The Great Un-making]].
- The [[Great Forge of Moradin]] & the [[Heartwood]] — A divine vision from [[Moradin]] reveals a primordial “First Forge” at the world’s heart and a flaw the Un-King seeks to strike; the task is to find and protect the [[Heartwood]] before it’s corrupted.
- [[The Verdant Canticle]] — A living wood-script promising renewal if the [[World Tree]] is healed.
- [[The Ashen Codex]] — A chained bark-tome whose prophecy shows the [[Carrion Tree]] under the [[Empty Star]].


# MAGICAL ITEMS

- [[Gauntlets of the Mountain Heart]] — Rare; strength-tuned gloves forged with rune-steel and glacier iron.
- [[Heartstone of the Jötun]] — A luminous shard once embedded in a giant king; known to bolster will and presence.
- [[Jarl's Rune of Command]] — Carved authority-stone used by giant jarls to compel obedience and anchor battle lines.
- [[Mantle of the Rime-Stalker]] — Frost-white pelt cloak that turns snow and ice into a hunter’s road.
- [[The Saga-Stone]] — A story-bound keystone that answers lore with echoing verse; a diplomat’s boon among giants.

# NPCs (Non-PC)

- [[King Illhoof]] — The Sundered King; pragmatic, grief-fractured ruler of [[Kaeldor]].
- [[Princess Anya]] — The Royal Sister; public face of the line, sharp-minded and denied succession by law.
- [[The Royal Lineage of House Fenmir|Queen Annelise]] — Deceased; her death during childbirth helped trigger the succession crisis.
- Earlier Kings of Fenmir: [[King Henry|Henry I “the Unifier”]], [[King Alaric|Alaric “the Just”]], [[King Gareth|Gareth “the Builder”]], [[King Odegar]], [[King Torsten]].
- [[Lich King|Prince Eldhoof Fenmir]] — The vanished firstborn; later appears in giant records as the [[Lich King|The Crownless Voice]] opposing the last benevolent giant king.
- [[Lich King|Valameer]] — The [[Lich King]] who seized [[The Land of Razuul]] and raised [[Valdrak]].
- [[Lord-General Marius]] — Commander of the [[Order of the Adamant Veil]]; scarred veteran, tactically brilliant, unyieldingly loyal.
- [[Magister Kaelan]] — Acting head of the [[Veil Warden]], severe and survival-minded.
- [[Elara Vance|High Steward Elara Vance]] — Civil administrator of [[Aeris Gard]]; fiercely protective of the common folk.
- [[Ser Korbin|Ser Korbin Vance]] (“[[Ser Korbin|The Porcelain Knight]]”) — Silver-bright duelist of the Adamant Veil; calls the undead “the Afflicted” and Stonebridge’s fog “the Somber Veil.”
- [[Ser Joren]] — Knight-escort in the Princess’s retinue.
- [[Alis Ashworth]] & [[Kaan Gritmaw]] — Paired protectors in the Veil’s service: the tempered blade and the storming axe.
- [[Borin Stonehand|Guildmaster Borin Stonehand]] — Leader of the [[The Stone-Exiles]] in [[Aeris Gard]], covertly hostile and engineering-minded.
- [[Warchief Ghor’ash]] & [[Chief Tinker Fizzlebang]] — The fist and the brain of [[Khar-Thul]]’s duumvirate.
- [[King Ymir]] — Enslaved giant-king of [[Old Jotenheim]], heart-shard visible through his chest.
- [[Oswin|Dread-Revenant Oswin]] — Valameer’s hunter of artifacts; leaves biting cold in pursuit.

# Session
- [[Session 0]] [[Valdrak]]’s Dream & the Defiant Bell — The party is cast into a nightmare echo of [[Valdrak]]’s fall, pursued by beholders across a ruin until they reach a bell tower where the stone-bound [[Theron|Prince Theron]] tolls a desperate alarm and the dead beg release.
- [[Session 1]] The Broken King [[Oswin]] & Northbound Tidings — The heroes confront corrupted King [[Oswin]] on his errand toward [[Old Jotenheim]]; he withdraws under [[Lich King|Valameer]]’s shadow. On the road they spy a sealed carriage bearing [[Kaan Gritmaw]], [[Alis Ashworth]], [[Ser Joren]], [[Ser Korbin]], and [[Princess Anya]] heading the same way.
- [[Session 2]] The Watch in the Woods & “[[Frozen Wake]]” An unnatural fog-serpent with red eyes presses the camp; magic and grit force it back. Dawn leads to [[Frost-Tide Landing]], where smoke and cold ash mark calamity at the ferry.
- [[Session 3]] Invitation of the [[Raven Queen]] — A woodland omen and a guide-creature draw the company toward an audience the Queen of Ravens herself has requested, should they accept her summons at first light.
- [[Session 4]] The [[Grotto of the Oathing Serpent]] & [[Himnir]] Unsealed — In the grotto, ice-shards and mephitic wings burst from the dark: 4–6 Ice Mephits and a lurking hunter strike as ancient wards around [[Himnir]] falter.
- [[Session 5]] Echoing Ambush & the Two Lines — Hunted by devils under [[Oswin]]’s banner, the group faces a razor choice at the mountain’s edge: hold the narrowway with [[Kaan Gritmaw]]’s steel, or leap through fog toward corrupted Giants and a slim chance beyond. Counsel clashes: [[Ser Joren]] for the stand, [[Kaan Gritmaw]] for the shield-wall, [[Alis Ashworth]] for the mad leap.
- [[Session 6]] The White Abyss & the Catching Mist — The cliff gives way under sword-wrought cataclysm; an avalanche swallows all—until the storm’s own blue nimbus bears the company downward, setting them on the path toward the [[Frozen Library]].
