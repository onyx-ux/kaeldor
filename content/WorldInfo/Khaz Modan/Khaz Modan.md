# Welcome to Khaz'Modan

Welcome is not a word etched into the stone of [[Khaz Modan]]. Banners can be burned and oaths can be broken, but the mountain endures, and it is the mountain that offers the city it's only greeting: a biting, ceaseless wind that howls with the memory of betrayal. To enter the dwarven hold is to step into a living tomb, a city that died and was painstakingly hammered back to life. The air within the great stone halls is warm, not with camaraderie, but with the grim, industrial heat of the forges that burn day and night. The legendary acoustics of the vault, once designed to carry the joyous songs of feasting and brotherhood, now only serve to amplify the mournful echo of a thousand hammers. It is the sound of a city forever sharpening its teeth.

The dwarves themselves move with a profound and heavy purpose. They are a people whose laughter has turned to iron in their throats. Their faces are impassive, carved from the same granite as their halls, and their eyes hold the cold light of a forge fire. They work, they build, they train, their entire existence a testament to the singular belief that what has been lost can never be regained, only avenged.

One does not find statues of ancient kings here. The pedestals where the Fenmir dynasty was once honored are now bare, the stone repurposed for fortifications. In their place stand stark, unadorned columns of obsidian, each one a monument to a clan lost during the war, or a regiment of warriors who fell defending the walls while the south sealed them in. Their art is the art of the grudge; vast, intricate murals of carved stone depict not glorious histories, but a single, defining moment: the [[#Great Sundering]], with a faceless king on a floating throne turning his back as the north burns.

To visit [[Khaz Modan]] is to walk through the halls of a memory that will not fade. It is to feel the oppressive weight of a people who survived the end of their world not by the grace of gods or kings, but by the sheer, unyielding force of their own bitterness. Here, history is not a story told—it is the stone beneath one’s feet and the steel being hammered on the anvil, and neither will ever forget.

## Khaz Modan (The Stone-Exiles)
The dwarves of [[Khaz Modan]] are physically lost beyond the [[Aegis of Aeris]]. Their leadership exists as a powerful, resentful faction of refugees within the capital.

* **Leadership:** Guildmaster [[Borin Stonehand]] and the council of the [[The Stone-Exiles|The Stone-Exiles]].
* **Description:** [[Borin Stonehand]] is a master engineer with eyes as hard as the granite he can no longer touch. He is calculating and patient, presenting a public face of cooperation while privately nurturing the deep grudge of his people. He sees [[Aeris Gard]] not as a sanctuary, but as an unnatural prison in the sky.
* **Alignment to the Throne:** Covertly Hostile. [[Borin Stonehand]] and the [[The Stone-Exiles|The Stone-Exiles]] pay lip service to [[King Illhoof]] but work to gain control of the city's infrastructure from within. Their ultimate goal is to amass enough influence to force the King to find a way to return the city to the ground, viewing the human king as the architect of their exile.
