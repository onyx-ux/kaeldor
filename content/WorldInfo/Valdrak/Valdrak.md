# Goodbye to Valdrak
You do not say "welcome" to [[Valdrak]]. You do not cheer its name nor seek its gates, for the gates are shattered and the name is a wound. To this place, one can only whisper "goodbye."

Goodbye to the true crown city of old [[Kaeldor/WorldInfo/Kaeldor]]. Long before [[Aeris Gard]] touched the sky, [[Valdrak]] was the continent's beating heart, a city not of magic, but of magnificent, impossible machinery. It was a world of wonder forged by the most unlikely of pairings: the meticulous, ancestral genius of the Dwarves and the chaotic, feverish ingenuity of the Goblins. Here, all races were welcome, not just in spirit, but in practice. To walk the streets of old [[Valdrak]] was to see a clockwork automaton, its gears whirring in perfect Dwarven harmony, serve a drink to a goblin inventor arguing with an elven scholar. It was to ride steam-powered lifts that ascended through the mountain to districts of breathtaking beauty, and to see the city lit not by spells, but by the steady, bright hum of captured lightning. [[Valdrak]] was a promise—that logic, invention, and collaboration could build a paradise on earth.

And at its core, in the deepest chamber where the mountain's pulse could be felt, was its soul. The [[Great Forge of Moradin]]. It was not merely a forge; it was the most sacred site in the Dwarven world, holy ground where the Soul-Forger himself was said to place his hand upon the anvil. The fire within was a fragment of the mountain's core, a flame that was never meant to be extinguished. It was here that the Dwarves of both [[Valdrak]] and [[Khaz Modan]] made their pilgrimages, to feel the hum of creation and to hammer steel in the presence of their god.

The fall of [[Valdrak]] was not a battle; it was a blasphemy. The mindless, shrieking hordes of the [[Lich King]] did not just break the city; they tore apart a masterpiece. The symphony of whirring gears was silenced by the gnawing of undead teeth. The light of invention was snuffed out by a tide of profane darkness. And in the greatest act of sacrilege, the holy fire of the [[Great Forge of Moradin]] was extinguished, its warmth replaced by the chilling cold of necromancy.

To walk through the ruins today is to walk through the corpse of a beautiful dream. Rusted automatons lie frozen in the streets, their limbs twisted into silent screams. The great steam-lifts are now just skeletal, metal waterfalls of ruin. And the [[Great Forge of Moradin]] is a desecrated, silent husk, its cold anvil radiating a palpable aura of grief and defilement.

This is why the Dwarves of [[Khaz Modan]] will never forgive. [[King Illhoof]] did not just abandon a city of allies; he allowed their most sacred temple to be violated and left to rot. He sealed away not just a fortress, but a testament to what the world could be.

So, goodbye, [[Valdrak]]. Goodbye to the city of the cog and the rune. Goodbye to the light of reason and the fire of creation. Your silence is the true price of the [[#Great Sundering]], and your memory is the deepest wound in the heart of this broken world.
