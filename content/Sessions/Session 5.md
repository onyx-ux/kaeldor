# Session 5
## Event 1: The Parting of Ways & The Echoing Ambush

This backs on from the previous session where the party was ambushed by the Ice Mephits and Ice Devils. During this session, the idea is to have the party pushed to the cliff edge. With a seemingly impossible jump ahead of them. The jump is 50ft. As they fight their way to the cliff edge, on the other side is the Corrupted Giants which have been lead here due to the overall noise and cliff damage which are on the opposite side. The party will then need to decide if they will continue to fight the Ice Memphits and Ice Devils or jump and fight the Ice Giants.

## The Choice of Paths
As the PCs are cornered, they see across the gap: three massive, Corrupted Frost Giants lumber into view, drawn by the thunder of battle. Their skin is marked with glowing necrotic runes, veins of violet-black energy crawling across their pale flesh. They are not immediately hostile to the devils—instead they roar, challenging anything that dares enter their territory.

The party now faces a branching decision, one of those perfect cinematic crossroads that defines character and campaign tone:
* **Stand their ground** against the Ice Devils and swarm of Memphits. This is the “bravery through attrition” choice. The battle will escalate as the ground itself gives way. Victory here is possible but costly, with exhaustion and resources drained. Possible twist: the cliff collapses regardless, hurling them into the abyss (segue to “jumping down” path).
* **Make the Impossible Jump** across the 50 ft chasm to confront the Corrupted Giants. PCs must combine creativity, magic, and athletics. Mechanics: A single DC 20 Athletics check is brutal; instead, turn it into a Skill Challenge. 3 successes before 3 failures. Allow use of spells (Misty Step, Dimension Door, Feather Fall), ropes, improvised poles, or even pushing a falling Ice Devil into the chasm to use as a “bridge.” If they succeed, they land on the opposite side, where the corrupted giants challenge them in a fresh battle.
* **Leap Downward** into the abyss below the cliff. This is the “wild card” option. The mist is unnatural, hiding something ancient. Falling damage is negated (or heavily reduced) by a mysterious updraft of frost-laden magic. They land in a hidden ice cavern — perhaps a giant burial ground or an ancient leyline chamber twisted by the [[Lich King]]’s corruption. This path can unlock lore and treasure, but also a lurking boss (maybe a bound remnant of a long-dead Jotun shaman).

## Roleplay Dialogue
### Princess Anya
She’s brave and stubborn, but still very young — she will instinctively reach for honor and survival, while masking her own fear.
* **Seeing the cliff and giants:** “By the Light… the gap is impossible. We can’t make that! [[Ser Joren]], there must be another way—tell me there is another way.”
* **If players consider jumping across:** “Fifty feet of nothingness… you’ll break your necks. And even if you make it—those giants are corrupted. I saw them once as a child, noble and proud. Now look at them. To face them would be to fight ghosts.”
* **If the party suggests going down:** “Into the abyss? Do you even hear yourselves? We do not leap into darkness and pray the ground will catch us. That is suicide, not strategy. Yet… if the ice devils press us further, perhaps even madness is better than a frozen death.”

### Ser Joren
The stalwart knight, pragmatic and grim, always focused on her safety first.
* **If players suggest standing ground against the devils:** “I’ll not abandon my post. [[Princess Anya]] stands here. That means I stand here, and I will hold until my last breath. If you intend to flee, then flee—but I will be her shield until the end.”
* **If players consider jumping across to fight giants:** “Giants are no safer than devils. You’d leap from fire into frost, from certain death into death most certain. Still… if you’re mad enough to try, I’ll go where Her Highness commands. My oath binds me, even unto folly.”
* **If players suggest leaping down into the abyss:** “You would gamble with your lives—and hers—on the whims of the dark? No. Better to die where we stand, with blades drawn, than vanish into a pit where no one will even know we fell.”

### Alis
Measured, analytical, always weighing risk. He’s the foil to [[Kaan Gritmaw]]’s hot-headedness and [[Ser Joren]]’s unshakable loyalty.
* **On the devils pressing them back:** “We cannot bleed them forever. Every wound, every spell we cast is wasted here. This is a battle designed to grind us down. If we do not change the rules, they will.”
* **On the giants across the gap:** “Giants can be bargained with—even corrupted ones. Devils cannot. If the leap is possible, it may yet be wiser than waiting to be carved apart.”
* **On the abyss below:** “Do you feel that updraft? The mist isn’t natural. Something lives down there. Perhaps worse than giants… or perhaps, just perhaps, an escape the devils have not foreseen. It would be madness… but a clever kind of madness.”
