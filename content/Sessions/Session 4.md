## Locations & Setting Details
**The Grotto of the Whispering Falls:** A pocket of impossible warmth nestled in the frozen tundra.
* **Sights:** A curtain of steaming water cascades down moss-slicked rocks into a clear, turquoise pool. The air is thick with a pearlescent mist that clings to the strange, pale, geothermal flowers growing in the rock crevices. The firelight from the camp catches in the mist, creating a soft, hazy glow that feels both intimate and isolating, as if this small sanctuary is the only place left in the world.
* **Sounds:** The dominant sound is the constant, soothing roar of the waterfall, a white noise that muffles all other distant sounds. This is layered with the cheerful crackle of Doric's cooking fire and the low murmur of conversation.
* **Smells:** The air is heavy with the scent of damp earth, hot minerals, and wet stone. Cutting through it all is the rich, savory aroma of roasting meat and herbs from Doric’s stew pot—a powerful scent of civilization and comfort.
* **Atmosphere:** The grotto begins as a place of profound relief, a haven from the biting cold and the weight of the party's mission. It feels safe.
