# Session 3: The Weight of Duty and the Whispering Falls
(The session begins immediately after the battle with the Frost Giants from the end of Session 2. The air is cold, smelling of pine and spilled blood. The party stands amidst the wreckage of the royal carriage and the bodies of the slain giants, alongside [[Princess Anya]], [[Ser Joren]], and [[Alis Ashworth]].)

## Event 1: An Uneasy Alliance
The adrenaline from the sudden, brutal fight begins to fade, replaced by the biting cold and the stark reality of the situation.
The royal carriage, their primary means of transport and shelter, is a splintered ruin, its horses having bolted into the unforgiving tundra during the chaos.
[[Princess Anya]], her face pale but her resolve unshaken, steps forward.
She assesses the party not just as saviors, but as potential assets.
Her voice, though strained, carries the authority of her station.
"You fought with honor and courage. My knights and I are in your debt. However, our mission is far from over. We must press on to [[Old Jotenheim]]."
[[Ser Joren]], ever the dutiful guardian, seconds her, though his gaze is wary.
He eyes the party, particularly their more rough-hewn members, with professional suspicion.
"The Princess's safety is paramount. Our path is dangerous, and our resources are now... limited. We could use the additional swords."
Anya offers the party a formal, if temporary, arrangement: be knighted as provisional members of the Royal Guard for the duration of this mission.
It is a practical offer, granting them legitimacy and purpose in the eyes of the Crown, and binding their fates together on the perilous road ahead.
As the party considers this, [[James]], the hardy ferryman-turned-guide from [[Frost-Tide Landing]], approaches.
He looks at the giant corpses, his expression grim.
"This isn't right," he mutters, his breath clouding in the air.
"Giants this far south, this aggressive... they're usually reclusive, peaceful folk. Something has stirred them. Something is deeply wrong in the north."
This observation hangs in the air, a chilling premonition of what lies ahead.
(This is a natural point for roleplaying. The party must decide whether to accept the Princess's offer. This is also where [[Blake]] and [[Princess Anya]] will have their first significant interaction since the party learned of his heritage. Anya, unaware of his true identity, might see a fleeting, familiar resemblance or a noble bearing in the young man, perhaps reminiscing aloud about a lost brother she barely knew.)
Assuming the party agrees, a new plan is forged from the wreckage of the old.
With the carriage destroyed, they cannot all continue. [[Kaan Gritmaw]] and [[Ser Korbin]] are tasked to return south on foot, to report the giant attack and the loss of the carriage to the King.
This leaves [[Princess Anya]], [[Ser Joren]], and [[Alis Ashworth]] to continue their journey to [[Old Jotenheim]] with their new, unlikely allies.

"The silence that follows the departure of [[Kaan Gritmaw]] and [[Ser Korbin]] is heavy, filled only by the biting wind whistling over the tundra. The splintered ruin of the royal carriage serves as a stark monument to the violence that just occurred. With [[Ser Joren]] taking the lead, his expression grim and his eyes fixed on the horizon, your newly-formed company sets out on foot. The path ahead is a bleak, unforgiving expanse of frost-covered ground and sparse, skeletal trees. Each step is a conscious effort against the crushing cold and the weight of your new duty."
"After a day of arduous travel, a strange and unsettling change begins to occur in the landscape. The relentless wind softens, losing its sharp, icy bite. The snow, which had been a uniform, crisp white, begins to recede, revealing patches of unnaturally vibrant green moss, pulsing with a faint, internal luminescence. The skeletal trees of the tundra give way to towering pines, but their needles are not the deep green of a healthy forest; they are an iridescent, silvery-blue that seems to shift in the light.
The air grows warmer, heavy with the scent of damp earth and blooming flowers you cannot see.
The silence of the tundra is replaced by a profound, listening stillness.
There are no bird calls, no rustling of animals in the undergrowth—it is the quiet of a place holding its breath.
The light filtering through the canopy takes on a soft, ethereal quality, like perpetual twilight.
This is not a natural forest; it feels like a dream of one."

From here, the party was invited into the forest, by the forest and a raven.
A force compelled them to move into it. As they went into the forest they were met with the [[Raven Queen]].
Providing them with further information on what they need to do.
Providing each other with backstory lore and directions to where to go.
