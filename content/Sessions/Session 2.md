## The Frozen Wake
**(Scene Setting: The Approach)**
The last week of travel has been marked by a creeping chill.
The vibrant greens of the southern heartlands have given way to hardy pine forests and rugged hills.
The air is thin and sharp in your lungs. You are nearing the northern artery of [[Kaeldor/WorldInfo/Kaeldor]], the ferry terminal known as [[Frost-Tide Landing]], the last bastion of organized trade before the wilds that border the [[Aegis of Aeris]] and the frozen wastes of [[Old Jotenheim]].
As you crest the final hill that overlooks the river valley, the journey’s weariness is burned away by a jolt of adrenaline.
Against the horizon, a thick, greasy plume of black smoke crawls into the sky, a stark wound against the pale blue.
The air, once crisp with the promise of the northern pines, now carries a bitter, acrid tang—the smell of wet, smoldering wood and something else... something colder.

**(Scene Setting: The Landing)**
Descending into the valley, you find [[Frost-Tide Landing]] is not in the midst of a battle, but in its grim, quiet aftermath.
The scene is one of calm panic. There is no screaming, no running for cover. Instead, the townspeople move with a frantic, desperate purpose.
Bucket brigades snake from the river to the smoldering remains of a large warehouse.
Families grimly load what few possessions they can salvage onto carts.
A handful of town guards, their faces smudged with soot, try to maintain order amidst the quiet chaos.
The source of the attack becomes chillingly clear upon your approach.
A path of unnatural destruction cuts a swathe directly through the town.
It is not a trail of fire and steel, but of absolute, life-draining cold.
Where this path runs, the very cobblestones are coated in a rime of black ice that does not melt, and the ground is a brittle, spectral grey.
It is the unmistakable trail of [[Oswin|Dread-Revenant Oswin]], the scar of his personal anti-life aura.
And pressed into the mud and snow alongside it are the clear tracks of a single, heavy carriage and its mounted escort.
The great river itself, the lifeblood of this town, is frozen solid near the docks not with the gentle crackle of a winter freeze, but in a sudden, violent sheet of jagged, unnatural ice.
The powerful ferries are trapped, their hulls groaning against the cold.
