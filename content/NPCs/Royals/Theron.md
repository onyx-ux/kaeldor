## Prince Theron Fenmir: The Young Lion
* **Description:** Even at fifteen, [[Theron]] carried the undeniable presence of a king. He had the sharp, noble features of the Fenmir line, but where his father [[King Illhoof]]’s eyes were filled with a diplomat’s warmth, [[Theron]]’s burned with a warrior’s fire. He was tall for his age, with a strength that belied his lean frame, moving with a swordsman’s coiled grace. His hair was the dark sable of his father's, but his charisma was entirely his own—a magnetic confidence that inspired loyalty in grizzled knights and drew the admiration of the court. He was not arrogant, but profoundly capable, and he bore the weight of his own potential with a solemn sense of duty. He was the kingdom's hope, the Young Lion of [[Aeris Gard]], and the perfect prince for a world on the edge of a knife.

Against the desperate pleas of his father and mother, [[Theron]] led a contingent of loyal knights north, a final, valiant charge to bolster [[Valdrak]]'s defence. But before he left, [[Theron]] did something that would alter the course of the dynasty. He slipped away to the hidden chambers where his youngest brother, [[Blake]], was kept in secret. To the young, confused child, [[Theron]], his shining older brother, was a figure of legend. In that secret meeting, [[Theron]] slipped the ancestral [[Royal Ring of Fenmir]] from his own finger and placed it upon [[Blake]]'s. "The line must endure," he whispered, a promise and a command. "Keep this safe for me. For the family." It was the last time [[Blake]] would ever see him.

## The Twist of Fate: The Petrified Prince
[[Theron]] and his knights reached [[Valdrak]] not in time to save it, but just in time to witness its damnation. The city was a maelstrom of chaos. While the main horde of the undead crashed against the [[Great Forge of Moradin]], a tide of bone and shadow seeking to extinguish its holy fire, the [[Lich King]] deployed his elite hunters to eliminate pockets of leadership and sow terror. [[Theron]] and his small retinue of loyal knights rallied the city guard at the Sky-Bridge Plaza, a vital chokepoint, cutting down swathes of ghouls and skeletons with the furious valor of a cornered lion.

But the [[Lich King]] did not send his rank-and-file to deal with the heroic prince. He sent his living siege engines of terror. From the smoke-choked sky, they descended. Beholders. But they were twisted, their flesh a bruised, necrotic purple, their eyestalks writhing with unnatural speed. In the center of each floated a great eye that burned not with their own malevolence, but with the cold, commanding blue light of [[Lich King|Valameer]] himself.

The knights, brave as they were, stood no chance. One was caught in a disintegration ray and screamed as he turned to dust. Another’s armor melted under an enervation ray before a death ray snuffed his life out. It was a slaughter. Soon, only [[Theron]] remained, a lone figure of defiance against the floating nightmares. He dodged, parried, and even scored a deep gash on one of the creatures with a heroic lunge, his sword blazing with the light of his conviction. For a breathtaking moment, the fifteen-year-old boy held the line.

But he was overwhelmed. The beholders focused their power, and a volley of shimmering grey rays shot from their eyestalks, converging on the prince. [[Theron]] raised his sword one last time, a final, defiant cry on his lips. His indomitable spirit and royal blood resisted the utter annihilation of the disintegration beams, but he could not defy the petrifying magic that washed over him. He was not slain, but frozen solid in his moment of greatest heroism—his face a mask of furious determination, his sword arm raised to strike, transformed into a perfect, stone-like statue. A monument to a courage the enemy could not break, only encase. His fate remains unknown to the world, which presumes him dead among the countless thousands. And so, somewhere in the haunted, silent ruins of [[Valdrak]], the petrified prince still stands, a forgotten hero waiting for a magic powerful enough to wake him from his stone slumber.
