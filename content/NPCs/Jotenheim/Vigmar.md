## Vigmar, Broken Sentinel

- **Role:** Once his brother and co-guardian of the seal. Now corrupted, the plague has rewritten him as a weapon of entropy. His life-force has been inverted, turned into a crack through which the Wanderer presses.
- **Appearance:** His body pulses with violet fractures, his skin split like shattered ice. His once-rune-carved hammer has splintered, replaced with a jagged shard of crystal glowing with necrotic light. Where Ornn emanates cold resolve, Vigmar radiates a suffocating emptiness.
- **Presence:** His voice is a fractured chorus — his own, Oswin’s whispers, and the humming imperative of the Wanderer: _Witness. Witness. Witness._
