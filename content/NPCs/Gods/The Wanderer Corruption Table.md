# Global Rule:

- The more a PC **uses** these temptations, the more visible their corruption becomes. After 3–5 uses, NPCs notice easily; after 7–10, clerics/paladins may refuse them aid; at 10+, they risk becoming a **thrall of the Wanderer** unless purified.

# Corruption Tracker: The Touch of the Wanderer

**How It Works**

- Each time a player uses a **corruption temptation**, they gain **1 Corruption Point (CP)**.
- Some narrative events (failing badly against the Pulse, touching relics, striking bargains with undead) may also grant CP at the DM’s discretion.
- Cleansing rituals, divine blessings, or powerful sacrifices can **reduce CP** — but this should feel rare and costly.

# Thresholds & Effects
## Corruption Tracker: The Touch of the Wanderer

| CP Range | Stage      | Narrative Effects | Mechanical Effects |
|----------|------------|-------------------|--------------------|
| 0        | Unmarked   | Scar is faint, barely noticeable. | Temptations usable freely. NPCs rarely notice. |
| 1–3      | Marked     | Scar visible in poor light; animals & children uneasy. | Advantage on Intimidation vs mortals; disadvantage on Persuasion vs devout NPCs. |
| 4–6      | Burdened   | Scar spreads; whispers louder; shadows act strange. | Advantage on death saves. Radiant healing restores half. |
| 7–9      | Profaned   | Nightmares every rest; reflections lag or stare back. | 1/day: auto-succeed a saving throw. Gain 1 permanent exhaustion until CP < 7. |
| 10+      | Vessel     | Marked as future thrall; holy wards burn you. | 1/day: void surge (max damage OR resist all dmg 1 rnd). Must pass Cha save (DC 18) or lose control briefly. |

## Purging or Resisting Corruption

- **Ritual Purge:** A holy ritual at a sacred site may reduce CP by 1d4 — but it requires rare materials and the risk of failure.
- **Acts of Sacrifice:** Selfless acts, renouncing temptation in dire straits, or suffering pain in silence can reduce CP.
- **Divine Intercession:** A god, archfey, or other higher power might intervene — but such aid always has its own price.

# Running the Tracker at the Table

- Don’t show them the full ladder. Let them feel the **symptoms** (scars deepening, whispers growing) as milestones.
- Keep corruption **tempting** — the powers are useful, and the costs don’t bite immediately.
- Make corruption **personal** — tie whispers or visions to their backstory, suggesting the Wanderer knows them.
- Allow cleansing, but keep it rare — falling too deep into corruption should feel hard to undo.
