_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_

This amulet is a chunk of unpolished, milky-white crystal, the size of a human fist, suspended on a thick iron chain. Within the crystal, a cold, blue light pulses softly, like a slow and powerful heartbeat. The stone is heavy with the weight of ages and feels ancient and unbreakable.

**_Lore:_** When the greatest of the giant elders, the Jötun, felt their time ending, they would retreat to the heart of a glacier. There, a Rune-Priest would perform a ritual to encase their heart, still beating with millennia of pride and willpower, in eternal ice. These "Heartstones" were worn by their successors, granting them the elder's unyielding spirit and indomitable presence, a will too vast and ancient to be swayed by the petty charms of lesser beings.

**_Mechanics:_** Your Charisma score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed.
