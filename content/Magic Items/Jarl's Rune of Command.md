_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_

A massive bronze clasp, the size of a shield, shaped into the angular, powerful rune for "Authority" (Vald). It was clearly meant to hold together the colossal furs of a Giant Jarl. It radiates an aura of absolute, unquestionable power and stern judgment.

**_Lore:_** A Giant Jarl's command was law. Their authority was not debated; it was simply obeyed. This rune was the symbol of that power. Worn by the ruler of [[Old Jotenheim]], it amplified their booming voice so it could be heard across a valley, turning a simple order into a decree that shook the very stones. Its magic is that of raw, intimidating presence, the social equivalent of a looming mountain.

**_Mechanics:_** Your Strength score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20. Additionally, you have advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks.
