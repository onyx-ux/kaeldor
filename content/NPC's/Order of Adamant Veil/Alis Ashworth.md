# Alis Ashworth

![[Alis.png]]


## Alis - A [[Order of the Adamant Veil|Knight of the Adamant Veil]]

 **Description:** In stark contrast to his companion, [[Alis Ashworth]] carries himself with a cool, coiled stillness. Where [[Kaan Gritmaw]] is a raging bonfire, [[Alis Ashworth]] is a tempered blade. He is leaner than his counterpart, with sharp, intelligent eyes that miss nothing. His movements are economical and precise, betraying a life of discipline and training. While just as wary of travelers as [[Kaan Gritmaw]], his wariness is tempered by a calm, analytical mind. He understands that not every stranger is a threat and that aggression is a tool to be used with precision, not a default state of being. His voice is even and measured, a calming counterpoint to [[Kaan Gritmaw]]'s volatile temper.

 **Mini-Backstory:** [[Alis Ashworth]] was once a soldier in the royal army, serving on the bleak frontiers where the whispers of the [[Lich King]] were more than just stories. He saw firsthand how fear and paranoia could rot a unit from the inside out. This experience taught him the value of a level head in the face of chaos. He was selected for the princess's personal guard not just for his skill with a blade, but for his unwavering composure. He is the strategist to [[Kaan Gritmaw]]'s brute force, the one who assesses a situation before drawing his weapon. While his loyalty to the crown is unquestionable, it is his personal dedication to [[Princess Anya]], whom he has watched grow from a solemn child into a determined young woman, that truly fuels his vigilance.
