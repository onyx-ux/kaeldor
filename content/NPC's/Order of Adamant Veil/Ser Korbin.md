## Ser Corbin Vance, "The Porcelain Knight"

* **Style of Speech:** [[Ser Korbin]] speaks with the flawless, almost melodic cadence of the [[Aeris Gard]] nobility. His tone is perpetually calm and measured, even in the heart of chaos. He describes the horrors he faces with a chilling detachment, using precise, almost clinical language as if cataloging specimens rather than fighting for his life. He refers to the undead not as monsters, but as "the Afflicted," and the nightly fog of [[Stonebridge]] as "the Somber Veil." His commands are never shouted; they are delivered with a soft-spoken authority that cuts through fear more effectively than any roar.
* **Lore:** [[Ser Korbin]] Vance is the younger brother of the city's pragmatic civil administrator, High Steward [[Elara Vance]]. While his sister fights battles with ledgers and supply chains to keep the common folk alive, Corbin wages a more direct war. He is the epitome of what the [[Order of the Adamant Veil]] represents: a breathtakingly beautiful soldier in mirror-polished armor, the flawless protector of a flawless city. Born after the [[#Great Sundering]], Corbin has never known a world where [[Aeris Gard]] was not in the sky. He was raised on stories of the harmony that was lost and the creeping rot that now seeps from the ground below. He descends with his retinue into the grime of [[Stonebridge]] during the dreaded "[[The Hour of Silence]]," not with grim determination, but with the focused grace of a master artist. The soldiers of the garrison and the huddled citizens of [[Stonebridge]] call him "The Porcelain Knight," a name born of both awe for his deadly grace and resentment for his untouchable, clean perfection. He moves through the grave-scented fog like a specter of light, his blade dispatching the creeping horrors with an efficiency that is both beautiful and terrifying to behold. He does not fight out of hatred for the enemy, but out of a fierce, protective love for the lie of [[Aeris Gard]]. He believes that beauty and order are the only things worth preserving in a broken world, and that the filth below must be scoured away, lest its stain reach the gleaming spires of his home. [[Ser Korbin]] Vance is the perfect knight for a gilded cage, a beautiful weapon who has never had to question if the thing he is protecting is worth the price being paid in the darkness below.
