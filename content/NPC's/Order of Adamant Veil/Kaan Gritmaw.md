# Kaan Gritmaw

![[Kaan.png]]

## Kaan - A [[Order of the Adamant Veil|Knight of the Adamant Veil]]
 
 ** **Description:** [[Kaan Gritmaw]] is a hulking figure, a mountain of a man whose presence alone is a warning. His hair is a wild, unkempt mane, and a thick, braided beard covers a jaw that seems permanently set in a scowl. His eyes are perpetually narrowed with suspicion, scanning the surroundings for any hint of a threat. He is clad in practical, heavy armor that has seen its share of battles, and a massive greataxe is never far from his grip. He moves with a gruff, impatient energy, quick to anger and fiercely protective of his charge. His voice is a low growl, and he speaks in short, clipped sentences, leaving no room for negotiation.

 **Mini-Backstory:** [[Kaan Gritmaw]] is a man forged in the harsh realities of a broken world. He hails from a remote village that was caught in the crossfire of warring factions years ago. He learned early that the only thing you can truly rely on is the strength of your own arm and the steel in your hand. This brutal upbringing has left him with a deep-seated distrust of strangers and a fierce, almost suffocating loyalty to those few he deems worthy of protection. He is not a man of noble birth or grand ideals; he is a survivor who has seen the worst of [[Kaeldor/WorldInfo/Kaeldor]] and will do whatever it takes to shield [[Princess Anya]] from it, even if it means becoming a monster himself. His loyalty to the princess is absolute, a silent oath sealed in the blood of those who have tried to harm her.
